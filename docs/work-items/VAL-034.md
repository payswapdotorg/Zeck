## Architecture note (within the work order's allowed surfaces)

`src/**` and `spec/**` are untouched (forbidden by this work order). The shadow platform slice lives in `benchmarks/validation/platform/shadow-execution.ts` (an allowed surface), mirroring the VAL-030..033 platform-slice pattern. The slice's PURE derivations: `deriveServingIsolation` (the served outcome must be the incumbent's — a served outcome sourced from the replacement, including a disguised leak, FAILs mechanically with the leak named; the shadow's outcome is observation-only), `deriveShadowPopulationCompleteness` (the shadow comparison must cover the FULL recorded traffic mix — a dropped case, a duplicated case or a mixed-up population FAILs), `deriveRegressionHonesty` (per-case agreement/divergence evidence — an aggregate-only divergence claim, an agreement asserted from a subset, a smoothed divergence or a false-divergence claim FAILs; every divergence is recorded case-by-case with both sides' digests), `deriveShadowCostSeparation` (the shadow's measured cost books to the shadow ledger APART from the served accounting — a double-booked or unmeasured shadow cost FAILs; the customer is never billed for the shadow), `deriveShadowStageDiscipline` (the transition `differentially-evaluated → shadow-executed` ONLY, with its evidence digest — any jump past shadow-executed, an evidence-less transition, a wrong-stage transition or a transition for a not-yet-differentially-evaluated candidate FAILs), `deriveShadowRefusalHonesty` (a refusal is honest only when the candidate is unregistered or not yet differentially-evaluated — a refusal that hides a shadowable candidate FAILs), the mid-shadow containment carry-over (the replacement still runs inside its granted isolation surface — an escape during shadow execution is a named containment violation that FAILs), the append-only divergence + shadow-cost ledgers, the FNV-1a digest discipline (payload-free), `deriveShadowRowCriteria` (the per-row mechanical verdict), and `verifyShadowExecutionAppContract` (the app-boundary oracle).

**The shadow honesty invariants this slice pins:** the served outcome is ALWAYS the incumbent's (observation-only shadow — any leak, disguised or not, FAILs); the regression comparison covers the full recorded traffic population with per-case evidence (aggregates and subsets FAIL); divergences are recorded case-by-case with both sides' digests, never smoothed (an honest FAILED row is a legitimate terminal); the shadow cost is measured separately and never billed to the served accounting; the lifecycle advances one evidenced rung (`shadow-executed`) and NEVER beyond (canary/promotion are VAL-035's scope); the VAL-032/033 registries and lifecycle history are read-only input (a different-content commit under a recorded identity THROWS); evidence carries payload DIGESTS, never payload bytes.

The app (`benchmarks/validation/apps/shadow-execution/`) declares the corpus (AC1): 12 offline rows + 1 env-gated live row. Per row the corpus declares the shadowed candidate (a VAL-033 differentially-evaluated lifecycle identity with its prior evidenced walk), the traffic population (citing the recorded replay populations), the expected shadow comparison (agreement / honest divergence mix with the divergence count pinned) and the expected stage transition. The corpus includes the four positive shadow rows (one per candidate kind — each shadowing the corresponding equivalence-tested replacement), the honest-divergence row (the removed-call replacement diverges on all 10 cases — FAILED honestly, its 10 divergences recorded case-by-case), the two honest refusals (unregistered candidate; premature candidate not yet differentially-evaluated), and six shadow-probe rows (leaked-outcome / dropped-case / smoothed-aggregate / billed-shadow / skipped-stage / mid-shadow-escape) whose populations are pinned now and whose adversarial fake-path variants FAIL mechanically in the discrimination suites. The app rides the public SDK boundary (`createZeckClient` — no `src/**` import), drives each row's shadow run through the harness's submit seam, reads the served outcome + divergence ledger + shadow-cost ledger + lifecycle landing back (RE-DERIVING the contract, never trusting the platform's claim), and verifies its own contract with the PURE `verifyShadowExecutionAppContract`.
