## Architecture note (within the work order's allowed surfaces)

`src/**` and `spec/**` are untouched (forbidden by this work order). The maturity platform slice lives in `benchmarks/validation/platform/determinization-maturity.ts` (an allowed surface), mirroring the VAL-030..035 platform-slice pattern. The slice's PURE derivations: `deriveMaturityEvidenceCitation` (every maturity claim cites its full evidence population — the lifecycle records, promotion generations and accounting measurements; an uncited claim, a phantom citation or a partial population FAILs with the members named), `deriveCurveSeriesIntegrity` (the generation series is GAPLESS and every point cites RECORDED measurements only — a missing generation, an unmeasured generation, an extrapolated or fabricated point FAILs and is named; each point carries its generation's FULL digest-relevant fact set so the boundary re-derivation reconstructs the recorded generation exactly), `deriveMaturityClassification` (the verdict must match the observed data — the four honest classes `determinized-stable` / `determinizing-trending` / `variable-resilient` / `immature-insufficient-evidence` over their pinned threshold shapes; each misclassification direction FAILs with both sides named; a trend or stability claim from insufficient generations FAILs as evidence-stretching — the family is classified immature, never stretched), `deriveSavingsReconciliation` (per-mechanism savings — reuse/cache/competence/deterministicization attributed separately — that reconcile with the recorded accounting ledgers; an aggregate-only number, a mismatch with both sides named, an inflated number or a non-summing breakdown FAILs), `deriveMaturityHonesty` (measured, never estimated; honest none-reported boundaries), the read-only history/accounting inputs, the append-only maturity-report ledger, the FNV-1a digest discipline (payload-free), `deriveMaturityRowCriteria` (the per-row mechanical verdict), and `verifyDeterminizationMaturityAppContract` (the app-boundary oracle).

**The maturity honesty invariants this slice pins:** every claim cites its full recorded population; the curve series is gapless with recorded points only (extrapolation is unrepresentable); the classification is faithful to the observed data in both directions; insufficient evidence is classified as what it is — never stretched into a trend; the savings reconcile per-mechanism against the recorded ledgers; the recorded history is read-only input (the facts digest is IDENTICAL after the whole corpus; a different-content commit under a recorded key THROWS); evidence carries payload DIGESTS, never payload bytes.

The app (`benchmarks/validation/apps/determinization-maturity/`) declares the corpus (AC1): 11 offline rows + 1 env-gated live row. Per row the corpus declares the workload family, the generation series (citing the recorded lifecycle + accounting evidence) and the expected maturity classification + curve facts. The corpus includes the four honest classes (rag-retrieval determinized-stable over 4 generations; text-summarization determinizing-trending over 4; order-settlement variable-resilient over 4; support-triage immature-insufficient-evidence over 2 — the honest evidence limit), five maturity-probe rows (uncited-claim / gapped-series / misclassified-family / unreconciled-savings / insufficient-evidence-trend) whose populations are pinned now and whose adversarial fixture variants FAIL mechanically in the discrimination suites, and two honest refusals (unrecorded family; no generations recorded). The app rides the public SDK boundary (`createZeckClient` — no `src/**` import), drives each row's analysis through the harness's submit seam, reads the maturity report back (RE-DERIVING the contract, never trusting the platform's claim), and verifies its own contract with the PURE `verifyDeterminizationMaturityAppContract`.
