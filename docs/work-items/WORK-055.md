## Recovery-strategy examples (why a strategy was / was not chosen)

- **Re-route chosen** (the representative corpus): after a transient `transport-unreachable` infrastructure failure on the incumbent route (rail-a/model-x), the strategy corpus evaluates retry-attempt-1 (400/0.95 → 421 µUSD), reroute-model (rail-b/model-y, 316), reroute-substrate (mid-container-b, 242) and escalate-fresh (638) — all sufficient against the 0.85 assurance floor and the budget ceiling, so the deterministic cost-ascending order selects **reroute-substrate at 242 µUSD** (the substrate-economics plane's own selected verdict, consumed read-only). Recorded as a `representation-substitution` decision with the attribution provenance in the basis.
- **Retry chosen** (identity-preserving): the same failure with only the retry path offered (no alternatives, no escalation) selects **retry-attempt-1** — the same representation re-executed, recorded with the `identity` basis; the retry discipline held (infrastructure + transient + attempts remaining + the claim meets the floors/ceilings).
- **Retry NOT chosen** (the discipline): an intelligence failure (verification FAIL, observed quality 0.4) with a retry fact offered → the retry verdict is inadmissible with `class-not-retryable` (retrying the same model on a quality failure is the runaway-token-inflation trap ADR-0020 forbids) — recovery goes to re-route or escalate-fresh, or fails closed. A budget-exhaustion resource failure → `budget-resource-not-retryable` (the budget authority stays the authority). A non-transient infrastructure failure → `retry-not-transient`. Attempts exhausted → `retry-attempt-bound` (bounded re-execution).
- **Escalation chosen** (evidence-driven): an intelligence failure with the fresh-escalation hook deciding `escalate-fresh-context` (the fresh path meets the floor and the continuation is degraded — WORK-053's own arithmetic, consumed) and the cheapest sufficient claim → `escalate-fresh` selected; the escalation package carries the attributed failure, the bounded evidence and the continuation payload.
- **Fail-closed** (zero-recovery, honest): no retry facts, no admissible alternative routes, no sufficient substrate, no justified escalation → `fail-closed`/`no-admissible-strategy` with every structural verdict recorded — NEVER a silent default retry, NEVER an invented fallback path, and NO decision record (the typed outcome is the evidence).
