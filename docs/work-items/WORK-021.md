## The deterministicization lifecycle (design decisions)

- **The contract's pipeline is a guarded state machine**: `proposed → validating → validated → shadow → canary → promoted | rejected | deferred | rolled-back` with a frozen forward-only transition table (`CANDIDATE_STATUS_TRANSITIONS`), enforced AGAIN in migration 0019 (single-step status trigger; terminal statuses frozen; `promoted` is deliberately NOT frozen — its only legal move is the rollback path to `rolled-back`; rows never deleted). `rolled-back` is terminal: re-validation is a NEW candidate (single-epoch rollout lifecycle — a fresh epoch never silently inherits an old one's evidence).
- **Learning records and gates; tools execute; the sandbox admits** (no second authority, anywhere): the deterministicization service deps are exactly the non-authority quartet `{store, digest, generateId, now}` — no policy, capability, budget, admission, sandbox or execution seam (scanner-pinned, D9); the replacement program's EXECUTION happens through the tools module's sandbox-executor (the WORK-018 synthesis-executor pattern); the sandbox module's own admission chain stays THE authority (nothing in learning can dispatch anything — the migration records evidence and decisions only, and no field of any record can mutate execution identity).
- **Discovery is advisory; the proposal is the durable act**: discovery reads the immutable telemetry population and mines recurring AI subgraphs WITHOUT writing anything; `proposeCandidate` is the first durable write, with content-derived identity (same basis ⇒ same row ⇒ `replayed` convergence), and a provenance-less proposal fails closed at the boundary AND is physically unrepresentable in 0019.
- **Write-once evidence, honest insufficiency**: one settled record per (candidate, stage) — a different basis for a settled stage is `IDEMPOTENCY_KEY_REUSED`; empty runs record honest `insufficient` (never fabricated); every run observation carries its sandbox-execution identity (validation runs are real sandbox executions, not claims); differential pairs are representable ONLY on the differential stage.
- **The promotion gate is a pure, fail-closed function** over recorded evidence (never a mutation of state): candidate + stage evidence + rollouts + config → `{verdict, reasons, evidenceIds, rolloutIds}`; the service BINDS the verdict (`evaluation.verdict !== "promote"` refuses to apply) and records the full evaluation in the decision journal; rejection/deferral are honest decisions with mandatory rationale (DTR-004); a candidate whose evidence passes the gate cannot be rejected (honest states only).
- **Rollout phases are single-epoch with measured deltas**: UNIQUE (application, candidate, mode) — one shadow epoch and one canary epoch per candidate; `observing → concluded` is the only move; the deltas (population, matchedCount, cost/quality/latency) are recorded at conclusion and frozen (concluded-rollout delta immutability, fixed after the PG proofs caught the gap); shadow-before-canary-before-promotion is structural in the service gates AND the promotion gate.
- **Rollback is an append, never a rewrite**: the rollback decision records the incumbent restoration target (`restoredIncumbent` = the incumbent binding's rollback target), the candidate moves to terminal `rolled-back`, the journal is append-only, and the consulted signal carries the restored-incumbent anchor so planning sees the restoration. The incumbent execution lineage stays canonical throughout (DTR-003).
- **Planning consumes through a consultation seam, never a state write**: the planner gained an OPTIONAL `deterministicizationSignals` dep (the consumer-owned port pattern); the consultation is captured AFTER the governed selection — post-selection evidence, never a live-route rewrite (pipeline order is the protection, D15); only a PROMOTED candidate carries the deterministic direction (the implied preference = the admissible candidate with the FEWEST generative steps; shadow/canary/deferred/rolled-back are divergence evidence with a null preference — the WORK-020 M-canary discipline); the capture records agreement/disagreement but `agreesWithSelection` is RECORDED, never applied (D18); every consulted candidate is re-validated fail-closed at BOTH the adapter and the consumer-side domain mirror (an unprovenanced candidate never enters a durable decision record, R7); the capture is part of the closed `PlanningDecisionRecord` shape. An un-wired planner is byte-identical to the baseline.
- **Crash safety is built-in, not bolted-on** (the WORK-024 review standard, applied from the start): every governed lifecycle operation (candidate-registration, stage-evidence, shadow-rollout, canary-rollout, promotion, rollback) owns ONE durable row in `learning.deterministicization_operations` with the `PENDING → COMPLETED | FAILED` machine, a monotonic `attempts` ledger, bounded stage checkpoints, and STABLE CONTENT-DERIVED operation keys (`dtr-${kind}:${discriminator}` where the discriminator is the content-derived durable identity — candidateId, evidenceId, rolloutId/`rolloutId:conclude`, decisionId — so a retry or a restart re-derives the SAME key); UNIQUE (application, operation_key) arbitrates the claim; the claim precedes the durable insert and the completion follows every durable outcome; idempotent transitions are computed by CURRENT-STATE RE-READ (never by trusting the insert outcome — the defect the C-proofs found and fixed); terminal rows replay their recorded outcome/failure with zero new side effects. Proofs: C1–C15 (unit kill/restart) + P1–P10 (real-PG kill/restart) — tables below.
- **Migration discipline (parallel wave)**: **WORK-021 claims 0019** — `0019_deterministicization_lifecycle.sql` (five learning-schema tables: `deterministicization_candidates`, `deterministicization_stage_evidence`, `deterministicization_rollouts`, `deterministicization_decisions`, `deterministicization_operations`), the claim PINNED in the file header; 0015 untouched (burned), 0016/0017/0018 untouched, 0020 left to the sibling WORK-025. Tenant scope is never dropped: every table carries `(application_id, tenant_id)` FK to `applications.applications`; evidence/rollout/decision rows FK to their OWN application's candidate (composite FK — the fix the PG proofs forced); the operations ledger's write-once core, terminal immutability, monotonic attempts and no-delete are trigger-enforced.
- **One refreshed directly-required gate**: `tests/integration/postgres/learning-schema.test.ts` inventories the five 0019 tables (nineteen learning tables total). No other stale gate needed changes.

## Crash-safety proofs (the WORK-024 review standard)

**Unit C-proofs** — `tests/unit/learning/deterministicization-crash-recovery.test.ts` (C1–C15). The crash model: a Zeck process dies mid-operation; the durable world (the learning store with its operations ledger) SURVIVES; a "restart" is a NEW service booted over the surviving world; the Proxy-based injector arms ONE durable-boundary crash point per process (a store method, before/after its durable commit, outside the best-effort `.catch()` regions, with fired-assertions against vacuous proofs); the test re-boots and re-issues the SAME logical operation:

| Lifecycle point | Proof records |
|---|---|
| REGISTRATION | C1 crash BEFORE the operation claim (nothing durable; the retry does the full work) · C2 crash AFTER the claim (row PENDING; the retry resumes the same key) · C3 crash AFTER the durable insert (the retry replays the durable outcome) · C4 DOUBLE crash (crash again on the retry's completion; the second retry converges) |
| STAGE EVIDENCE | C5 crash AFTER the evidence insert (ONE evidence record, ONE operation) · C6 crash AFTER the status transition (converges through the idempotent tail) · C7 crash AFTER the checkpoint write (the checkpoint survives; the restart completes) |
| ROLLOUT | C8 crash AFTER the rollout insert (ONE rollout, ONE operation, status moved) · C9 crash AFTER the conclusion write (replays the concluded deltas — first writer wins) |
| PROMOTION | C10 crash AFTER the decision append but BEFORE the status move (ONE decision, promoted) · C11 crash AFTER the status move (the retry replays the RECORDED decision — the gate is never re-run) |
| ROLLBACK | C12 crash after the rollback decision append (converges; the incumbent restoration is durable) |
| LEDGER DISCIPLINE | C13 a durably FAILED operation fails CLOSED on retry (the typed failure replays) · C14 the attempts ledger is honest across REPEATED crashes (monotonic, one bump per process) · C15 a COMPLETED operation replays with ZERO new durable side effects |

**Real-PG P-proofs** — `tests/integration/postgres/deterministicization-crash-recovery.test.ts` (P1–P10) over migration 0019 on real PostgreSQL 16.4, with `deterministicization-world.ts`'s `boot(point)` process-restart primitive (crash-injecting proxies over the PG store):

- P1 REGISTRATION: crash after insertCandidate — the candidate row survives; the restart converges the re-propose and completes the operation.
- P2 REGISTRATION double crash: two successive dying processes leave the attempts ledger honest; the third boot completes.
- P3 STAGE EVIDENCE: crash after insertStageEvidence — the evidence row survives; the restart converges the re-record AND lands the status tail.
- P4 STAGE EVIDENCE: crash after the operation checkpoint — the checkpoint survives; the restart resumes and completes.
- P5 SHADOW ROLLOUT: crash after insertRollout — the observing rollout survives; the restart converges and moves the tail.
- P6 SHADOW CONCLUSION: crash after concludeRollout — the concluded rollout survives; the restart converges on the committed row.
- P7 PROMOTION: crash after appendDecision — the decision survives; the restart re-derives the same decisionId and converges (exactly ONE promotion).
- P8 PROMOTION REPLAY: crash after the promoted transition — the restart NEVER re-runs the gate; the recorded decision is the authority and the crash-window PENDING operation row converges.
- P9 ROLLBACK: crash after appendDecision — the rollback decision survives; the restart converges (exactly ONE rolled-back decision; the incumbent restoration is durable).
- P10 DISCIPLINE: the operations ledger — pending-only attempts bump, terminal immutability, core immutability, no delete.

**Stable idempotency key scheme**: every operation key is `dtr-${kind}:${discriminator}` with the discriminator being the CONTENT-DERIVED durable identity of the operation's target (the candidate id for registration, the stage-evidence id, the rollout id / `${rolloutId}:conclude` for rollouts, the decision id for promotion/rejection/deferral/rollback) — a re-derived key is by construction the SAME key (exactly one durable operation row and one outcome set per logical operation, ever); the scheme is application-scoped with the tenant bound on the row (an operation key claimed by another application scope fails closed `TENANT_SCOPE_VIOLATION`).

**Defects the proofs found and fixed during the increment** (disclosed — the proofs earned their keep): (1) the converged-insert status-tail skip — an idempotent replay trusted the durable insert's outcome and skipped the lifecycle status tail; transitions are now computed by CURRENT-STATE RE-READ (C6); (2) the journal-then-fail admission-denial mapping — the sandbox admission throws its typed denial WITH a durable DENIED row; the executor originally propagated the error and lost the durable provenance; the denial is now an honest recorded failure observation (RS6); (3) the SQL store's composite-FK uniqueness, the promoted-not-terminal rollback path and the concluded-rollout delta immutability — three physical gaps in 0019 that only the real-PG proofs exercised; (4) `rolled-back` was made terminal (single-epoch rollout lifecycle, re-validation is a new candidate) when the discrimination suite proved the re-entry path would blur epochs.

## Limitations

- **No production consumer of the lifecycle is wired yet**: the service + stores + seams are module-level public contracts (the same stage WORK-014/017/020/022 shipped at); the planner seam and the executor are wired in tests today — there is no HTTP route, no operator UI, and no composition-root wiring that feeds observed execution telemetry into candidate proposals automatically. An operator (or a future experience layer) drives `propose → record → roll out → decide`.
- **Validation evidence is RECORDED, not computed by the lifecycle itself**: the composition supplies the observed runs (real sandbox-executor observations) for each stage; the lifecycle enforces the shape, the write-once discipline, the honest status vocabulary and the gate, but it does not itself synthesize the replay corpus or the differential harness — the acceptance criterion "validate using replay/differential/property/metamorphic testing" is satisfied by the enforced evidence contract + the real-execution proofs (RS1–RS8 run REAL replacement programs), not by an automated harness generator.
- **Shadow/canary "rollout" is the evaluation lifecycle, not live traffic splitting**: the phases record measured deltas over an observed population supplied at conclusion (the honest count of matched/cost/quality/latency observed by the composition); there is no per-request dual-execution or traffic-percentage runtime in v1. The planner records what a canary WOULD prefer (divergence evidence, R6) — live shadow traffic comparison is future experience-layer work.
- **The v1 replacement-program language is the frozen pure-compute JavaScript subset** (the WORK-018 synthesis language; bounded source/input sizes; no network by default, allowlist egress only with granted hosts): richer languages (WASM, SQL, typed DSLs) are future executor versions; a removal-class candidate carries no program at all.
- **The promotion thresholds are shipped conservative defaults** (replay/differential floors 20, acceptance 0.95, property pass 1.0, canary match 0.99, quality degradation 0): an application must consciously relax them via the per-request gate override; the config validator rejects nonsense (D5) but cannot judge domain-appropriateness.
- **Canary quality-degradation bound assumes qualityDelta is the matched fraction** (matchedCount/population) — the two threshold checks (`minimumCanaryMatchRate` and `maximumQualityDegradation`) are two views of the same recorded fraction in v1; a separately-measured quality metric is future work.
- **CI runs no real-PG suites** (the standing WORK-002 convention): the PG proofs are local-verified against real PostgreSQL 16.4 and recorded above; the GitHub `Repository Governance` workflow covers governance/typecheck/lint/architecture/unit/integration (PG-skipped by design).
- **The known baseline quirks are recorded, not fixed**: the wave-3 activation living outside the frozen base (local governance reads the pre-activation state — expected green) and the WORK-019 tree absence are Architect-owned reconciliation items (disclosed in Pickup state above).
