## Architecture note (within the work order's allowed surfaces)

`src/**` and `spec/**` are untouched (forbidden by this work order). The discovery platform slice lives in `benchmarks/validation/platform/learning-discovery.ts` (an allowed surface), mirroring the VAL-030/031/026 platform-slice pattern. The slice's PURE derivations: `deriveEvidenceCitationCompleteness` (every cited digest must be a member of the recorded population AND every generalization must cite its FULL population — an uncited proposal FAILS, a partial population FAILS, a fabricated citation FAILS with the fabricated members named), `deriveCandidateKindFidelity` (the proposal's kind must match the mined structure — each direction of mismatch FAILs), `deriveConservatism` (a VARYING segment is NEVER proposed as a deterministicization candidate — a variance-smoothing proposal FAILs mechanically), `deriveRefusalHonesty` (a refusal is honest only when the recorded structure justifies it — a refusal that HIDES learnable structure FAILs; a refusal XOR proposal violation is a malformed outcome that FAILs), `deriveNoApplication` (the frozen-input digests before/after plus the registry facts — the discovery PROPOSES, never APPLIES), the registry landing (ONLY a verified proposal is recorded, at the `proposed` lifecycle stage, never an applied stage), the FNV-1a digest discipline (payload-free), `mineLearnableStructureOf` + `deriveHonestDiscoveryOutcome` (the honest miner: reuse shapes that span workloads, cache transforms with stable input→output digests, competence step-patterns, stable segments — and the three honest refusals `varying-population` / `no-dispatched-work` / `no-learnable-structure`), `deriveLearningDiscoveryRowCriteria` (the per-row mechanical verdict), and `verifyLearningDiscoveryAppContract` (the app-boundary oracle).

**The discovery honesty invariants this slice pins:** evidence citations are checked for MEMBERSHIP (a phantom digest or identity FAILs) and COMPLETENESS (a stability claim from a partial replay set FAILs); candidate kinds are faithful to the mined structure; conservatism is absolute over variance; a refusal is justified by the recorded structure or it FAILs; only verified proposals land in the registry, at `proposed`, and the landing is exactly-once (an identical re-proposal REPLAYS its immutable identity; a different proposal under a recorded id is REFUSED); the frozen ledger inputs are byte-identical before and after every run (the no-application proof); evidence carries payload DIGESTS, never payload bytes.

The app (`benchmarks/validation/apps/learning-discovery/`) declares the corpus (AC1): 10 offline rows + 1 env-gated live row. Per row the corpus declares the mined evidence population (trajectory digests + replay identities from VAL-031's recorded ledger), the candidate kind (reuse/cache/competence/deterministicization), and the expected proposal (or the honest refusal reason). The corpus includes the four positive populations (one per candidate kind — the reuse population spans workloads), the two honest-refusal populations (the varying order-settlement population and the oversized-batch guard), and five discovery-probe rows (uncited / partial / fabricated / variance-smoothing / state-mutation) whose populations are pinned now and whose adversarial fake-miner variants FAIL mechanically in the discrimination suites. The app rides the public SDK boundary (`createZeckClient` — no `src/**` import), drives each row's discovery run through the harness's submit seam, reads the outcome back, and verifies its own contract with the PURE `verifyLearningDiscoveryAppContract`.
