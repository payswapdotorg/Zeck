## What this order IS

The E1.1 model-economics plane — the economics layer ADR-0020 designates for adaptive model/effort/agent selection — over the WORK-049/050 foundation: a new platform plane (`src/platform/model-economics/`, 9 modules, 2,822 engine lines; 7,094 new lines including tests) whose every selection is a **pure function of (plan IR, candidate facts, quality facts, constraints)**, fail-closed on unmet preconditions, with:

- **quality-aware model selection** (`selectModelRepresentation`): the least expensive SUFFICIENT route among declared candidates — admissibility first (the hard-constraint floor: quality, reliability, budget, latency, policy-route), then the least expected successful-resolution cost (the WORK-049 cost model arithmetic) among admissible candidates, with deterministic tie-breaking (total comparator; candidate-id order);
- **reasoning-effort selection** (`selectReasoningEffort`): the same explicit-basis economics over the closed five-level effort ladder (minimal/low/medium/high/maximum);
- **the 0/1/N agent gate** (`selectAgentStrategy`): zero-agent (the cheapest sufficient path — cache reuse, deterministic computation), one-agent, or N-agent — N requires a **positive expected net gain** (quality gain monetized through the economics' `qualityValueMicroUsd`, minus the agent fleet cost and the verification burden of divergent outputs); always-on N is impossible by construction (three independent discrimination proofs — D2);
- **expected-gain trade-off machinery** (`analyzeExpectedGain`): bounded, typed, total computations of expected quality gain versus cost/latency/verification burden — a gain that does not clear the threshold is `quality-gain-below-threshold` (inadmissible), never silently accepted;
- **service-class selection hooks** (`selectServiceClass`): hooks only, over the declared three-class vocabulary (economy/standard/priority) where the substrate supports them;
- **fresh-escalation decision hooks** (`decideFreshEscalation`): records when escalation-to-fresh-context is economically justified (strictly cheaper expected successful-resolution cost at equal-or-better quality/reliability, or quality/reliability justification above the hard floors); the recovery behavior itself is WORK-055's — this order only records the decision;
- **the decision-record construction seam** (`decisions.ts`): all five selections materialize as WORK-049-format decision records (`buildModelDecisionRecord`, `buildEffortDecisionRecord`, `buildAgentGateDecisionRecord`, `buildServiceClassDecisionRecord`, `buildFreshEscalationDecisionRecord`) — content-addressed, digested, evidence-honest (the below-assurance candidates are recorded WITH their invalid evaluations — the durable "why the cheaper did not win"), validated by the foundation's total read-time validation, and appended through the EXISTING store at the caller's seam;
- the closed, typed vocabulary (`vocabulary.ts`): nine invariant codes, bounded micro-USD arithmetic (overflow-safe `bigint` parsing, the ceiling 999,999,999,999,999,999 µUSD), agent counts bounded 2..256 (N-agent means ≥ 2 by definition — zero and one are the other gate modes), provider-neutral route references (no vendor vocabulary — C2).

**No new authority, no router, no provider registry, no ambient model state, no new durable surface (zero migrations — the count stays 29), no execution-lifecycle modification, no live provider call, no second state machine.**

## Required checkpoints

- **VERIFICATION-SEPARATION** — the required outcome quality is a HARD FLOOR, and the plane proves it in the evidence-honest split: `governingFacts` derives `hardQualityFloor` (the maximum hard `quality`-constraint floor carried by the governing constraints — below it, a candidate is record-EXCLUDED `quality-below-hard-floor`) separately from `assuranceThreshold` (the Work Order's `requiredQuality` — below it but above the hard floor, a candidate is recorded WITH its invalid evaluation, the durable "why the cheaper did not win"). Discrimination **D1**: below-floor selections are inadmissible regardless of cost across every floor dimension (quality, reliability, budget ceiling, latency ceiling, policy-forbidden route) — the mutation that lowers the floor to admit a cheap candidate is discriminated. The verification-anchor constraint is carried verbatim into every record's constraint corpus (never interpreted).
- **ECONOMIC-AUTHORITY-BOUNDARY** — every selection is decision evidence only: the plane holds no admission/authorization vocabulary (architecture **C5**, discrimination **D4** — no authorization surface exists anywhere on the plane), no store, no SQL (C4), and the durable append is the caller's seam through the EXISTING WORK-049 store. No runtime authorization path consults model/effort/agent selection (C3: no module, integration or api file references the plane). No routing authority: no router, no provider registry, no ambient model state (C4 + C2 provider neutrality).
- **DEPENDENCY-DIRECTION** — the plane imports ONLY `../execution-ir/*` (the WORK-049 foundation: decision-record, cost model, constraints, IR, audit, canonical), its own local modules and node builtins (architecture **C1**: the per-file import-prefix proof + the repository-wide dependency-rule scanner); the compiler's representation-ladder hooks are consumed import-only (the plane never edits the engine or the foundation — the C-final foundation-untouched proof); no module depends on the plane for authority (C3).
- **IDENTITY-IDEMPOTENCY** — re-selecting under identical facts is a bounded no-op: the selections are pure functions, the records are content-addressed over canonical forms, and the store's unique index + ON CONFLICT DO NOTHING serialize replays. Unit: byte-identical records across double selection; integration: deterministic re-selection (identical `decisionId`/`recordDigest`, `replayed: true`) and **N=8 simultaneous select+append chains over real PG converge to exactly one durable row** (one insert, seven replays, no duplicates, no torn records — the concurrency-crash-safety proof). Different decisions coexist durably (the five-record integration proof).
- **EXECUTION-PROVENANCE** — the exact chain plan → IR → selection → decision record: every record references the governed `planId`/`irId` (the derived-IR identity, verified through the planning seam), the full candidate corpus with per-candidate validated claims and evaluations, the governing constraints carried verbatim, the selection basis and the transformation basis from the CLOSED WORK-049 vocabulary (`identity`/`representation-substitution`); the whole chain is replayable by deterministic re-selection, and `auditDurableExecutionProvenance` audits the model-economics-produced record with ZERO violations over the real store (integration proof: plan → IR → decision, `derivedIrId` equal, no violations).
- **COST-QUOTA-GUARDS** — every quality/cost/latency claim is explicit-basis and bounded: the closed claim validation (basis `observed`/`estimated`/`defaulted` + bounded attribution source, bounded micro-USD within the closed ceiling, bounded quality/reliability in [0,1], bounded latency) rejects unattributed or unbounded claims with typed `claim-invalid` errors — discrimination **D3** proves both rejection families; the expected-gain arithmetic is total and typed (`bigint` micro-USD, bounded fractions, overflow-safe — `claim-overflow` on the ceiling breach); budgets and ceilings are GOVERNING (hard constraints from the captured policy inputs), not suggestions — `budget-ceiling`/`latency-ceiling` inadmissibility is proven (D1).
- **CONCURRENCY-CRASH-SAFETY** — concurrent selections of the same governed facts are pure recomputations converging on the identical `decisionId`; concurrent appends serialize through the WORK-049 store's unique index + ON CONFLICT DO NOTHING + locked re-read: N=8 over real PG → exactly one durable row, one insert, seven replays, no duplicates, no torn records (integration proof); the five distinct decisions coexist under one governed plan without interference.

## Selection decision examples (why a cheaper representation was / was not chosen)

- **Cheaper NOT chosen — below assurance (integration, real PG)**: `rail-b-model-y` claims 200 µUSD / quality 0.85 and `rail-b-degraded` claims 150 µUSD / quality 0.82 (both `estimated`, `planning.route-table`) — both above the captured policy hard floor 0.8 but BELOW the assurance threshold 0.9 → both inadmissible (`quality-below-assurance`) regardless of price, and both are recorded in the durable corpus with their invalid evaluations. `rail-a-model-x` (1,000 µUSD, quality 0.92, reliability 0.9 → expected successful-resolution cost `ceil(1000/0.9)` = **1,112 µUSD**) is the least expensive SUFFICIENT route and wins. Price never overrides quality.
- **Cheaper chosen (integration, real PG)**: the zero-agent cached path (50 µUSD, quality 0.92, `observed` basis from learning telemetry) wins the agent gate over the one-agent route (200 µUSD) and a positive-net-but-costlier N-agent candidate (500 µUSD fleet cost) — the cheapest sufficient path is the default, N needs its positive expected-gain evidence and loses on cost here.
- **Escalation justified (integration, real PG)**: the fresh context (1,000 µUSD, quality 0.95, reliability 0.95) is strictly cheaper at equal-or-better quality than the continuation (2,000 µUSD, reliability 0.9) → `escalate-fresh-context` is RECORDED (the decision evidence; the recovery behavior is WORK-055's).
- **No admissible candidate (unit)**: when every candidate falls below the hard floor, the selection is the typed no-admissible-candidate outcome and NO record is built — never a below-floor selection (D1).
- **N rejected for missing evidence (unit + D2)**: an N-agent candidate without a validated gain claim, or whose monetized net gain is non-positive after the fleet cost and verification burden, is inadmissible — always-on N is impossible by construction.
