## Architecture note (within the work order's allowed surfaces)

The work order forbids `platform/**`, `shared/**` and every sibling app's directory — the slice is strictly additive inside `benchmarks/validation/apps/economic-adjusted-cost/**`, mirroring the control-arm layout. The app rides the `ValidationHarness` boundary and imports (never modifies) the VAL-040 substrate (`protocol`/`normalization`/`pricing`/`driver`) and the three arms' recorded corpora.

**The synthesis oracles (all in the app-local driver, PURE):** `deriveQualityAdjustmentHonesty` (the denominator is the VERIFIED quality attainment — a quality-inflated denominator FAILs named with both sides), `deriveLatencyAdjustmentInclusion` (cost weighted by latency-budget compliance — an omitting or short-listed latency set FAILs named; stop-prefix latencies ride in), `deriveFailureAdjustmentCompleteness` (retries and failed attempts fully amortized — a hidden failed-round cost or a zeroed retry-overhead FAILs; NULL never zero), `deriveEstimateMeasureSeparation` (estimates conflated into measured facts FAILs named against the recorded basis), `deriveConfidenceAndMinimum` (Wilson intervals on every comparison; minimum sample sizes enforced; below-minimum claims FAIL; post-hoc arm exclusions and undeclared inputs FAIL), and the input-integrity oracle (every input digest-verified against its RECORDED arm corpus — a re-measurement masquerading as synthesis FAILs field-by-field; a digest-disagreed or unresolvable reference FAILs; an unpinned price revision FAILs). Every adjusted comparison carries the pinned price revisions of its inputs and the pre-registered arm set.

The corpus declares, per row: the recorded arm inputs (digest references into the VAL-041/042/43 corpora + the platform runs), the adjusted family, the pre-registered arm set, the statistical minimums + Wilson config, and the expected comparability verdict. 7 honest rows (the three headline family syntheses — comparable; two honest NULL incomparabilities — zero resolved/attainment pools; the honest stop-prefix; the honest below-minimum REFUSAL where the refusal IS the verified outcome) + 6 adversarial probes pinned as recorded data (quality-inflated denominator, latency omission, failure hiding, estimate conflation, re-measurement masquerade, below-minimum comparability claim) + 1 env-gated live row (honest NOT RUN).
