## Architecture note (within the work order's allowed surfaces)

The attribution platform slice lives in `benchmarks/validation/platform/savings-attribution.ts` (an allowed surface — this is session A's domain WO, mirroring the VAL-030..036 platform-slice pattern). The slice's PURE derivations: `deriveAttributionEvidenceCitation` (every attribution claim cites its full evidence population — the exact ledger entries, lifecycle records and cost measurements; an uncited claim, a phantom citation or a partial population FAILs named), `deriveNoDoubleCount` (each ledger entry attributed to AT MOST ONE mechanism — a double-count FAILs with BOTH claims named), `deriveResidualHonesty` (the unattributed residual is REPORTED, never forced into a mechanism — a residual claimed as reuse/cache/competence/deterministicization without that mechanism's recorded evidence FAILs named), `deriveAttributionReconciliation` (the identity — attributed per mechanism summed + the honest residual EQUALS the recorded total savings per family per generation; a non-reconciling attribution FAILs with both sides named), `deriveCounterfactualFidelity` (each mechanism's savings measured against the RECORDED incumbent baseline per VAL-044's adjusted model — an unstated, swapped or hypothetical baseline FAILs named), `deriveGenerationSeriesIntegrity` (the attribution's generation series matches VAL-036's recorded maturity curves — a gapped or mismatched series FAILs), the honesty/refusal oracles, the read-only inputs port, the append-only attribution-report ledger, the FNV-1a digest discipline (payload-free), `deriveAttributionRowCriteria` (the per-row mechanical verdict), and `verifySavingsAttributionAppContract` (the app-boundary oracle).

**The attribution honesty invariants this slice pins:** every claim cites its full recorded population; no ledger entry is attributed twice (both offenders named); the residual is honest — never forced into a mechanism; the reconciliation identity holds per family per generation; the counterfactual is the recorded incumbent baseline (per the adjusted-cost model), never a favorable hypothetical; the generation series matches the maturity benchmark's curves; the recorded history is read-only input (the facts digest IDENTICAL after the whole corpus; a different-content commit under a recorded key THROWS); evidence carries payload DIGESTS, never payload bytes.

The app (`benchmarks/validation/apps/savings-attribution/`) declares the corpus (AC1): 12 offline rows + 1 env-gated live row. Per row the corpus declares the workload family, the generation series (citing the recorded lifecycle + maturity evidence), the pinned mechanism evidence and the expected attribution (the per-mechanism split + the honest residual). The corpus includes the five honest split shapes (rag-retrieval all-mechanisms 400 µ$ attributed + 95 µ$ residual; text-summarization 90 µ$ attributed + a LARGE honest 320 µ$ residual; code-search cache-dominant; invoice-extraction reuse-dominant; translation-glossary deterministicization-dominant), five attribution-probe rows (uncited-claim / double-count / forced-residual / swapped-baseline / gapped-series) whose populations are pinned now and whose adversarial fixture variants FAIL mechanically in the discrimination suites, and two honest refusals (an unrecorded family; a family with no recorded savings). The app rides the PUBLIC SDK boundary (`createZeckClient` — no `src/**` import).
