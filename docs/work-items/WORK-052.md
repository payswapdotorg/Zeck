## Optimization decision examples (why a cheaper representation was / was not chosen)

- **Cheaper chosen** (integration, real PG): the duplication-accounting record for the coalesced group — `fresh-execution` claims 1000 µUSD / quality 0.92 / reliability 0.9 (basis `estimated`, source `planning.route-table`) → expected successful-resolution cost `ceil(1000/0.9)` = **1112 µUSD**; the `coalesce-led-execution` representation claims 10 µUSD / quality 0.92 / reliability 1.0 (basis `observed`, source `context-economics.cache-observer`) → **10 µUSD**. Both meet the 0.8 threshold; the reused/coalesced representation wins on expected successful-resolution cost — the record carries both claims with their bases and the role-specific outcome detail (joiner count, equivalence key, leader identity).
- **Cheaper NOT chosen** (unit, quality-preserving economics): a reuse claim with quality 0.5 against a 0.8 threshold — the accounting record is REJECTED before it exists (a typed `DecisionValidationError`: the selection would be a below-threshold reuse) — price never overrides quality, and a dishonest reuse record cannot be created.
- **Fail-closed negative decisions** (never errors): a stale fact (age 120s > 60s bound) → `compute` with `freshness-expired` (the stale value is NEVER served); a policy denial → `compute` with `policy-reuse-denied`; no fact → `compute` with `freshness-no-fact`; a step without a hook → `compute` with `semantics-no-hook`. The negative decisions are recorded evidence, so a caller can never misread a denial as permission or an absence as silence.

## Known limitations (honest boundaries)

- **Live provider/substrate economics: NOT RUN** — out of scope for this order (and for this worker environment, which holds no provider credentials). All cost claims (context pricing, fresh-execution and reuse economics) are explicit-basis INPUT fixtures carrying the `observed`/`estimated`/`defaulted` attribution contract — exactly the seam later E1.1 stages (WORK-053/054/056) wire to real telemetry.
- **The memoization-hook corpus under the real planner is currently empty** (the honest note above): the real planner's deterministic plans are fully verification-anchored, and the FIXED WORK-050 annotation contract emits hooks only on strategy-free deterministic steps. The consumer, planner and accounting chain is proven over authored plan shapes carrying real compiler-emitted hooks; when the planner emits such shapes, the same chain applies unchanged. The annotation contract itself was never touched.
- **Durable cache/memo ENTRIES are not implemented by this plane — deliberately.** The Work Order's duplication accounting rides the EXISTING decision-record store at the caller's seam; a durable memo-entry store (cache facts as a durable surface with TTL sweeps) is not in this order's Declared Change Surfaces (it would be a new migration and a new durable surface — a later order's decision). Cache facts are read-only inputs the caller provides; the plane proves the decision/evidence discipline over them.
- **The in-flight coalescer is process-local.** Cross-process coalescing (multiple service instances deduplicating equivalent in-flight work) would need a durable coordination surface — also outside this order's declared surfaces (and deliberately: a durable coordinator would edge toward a second execution lifecycle). The DECISION (equivalence, leader selection, age windows) is pure and rides the executions seam read-only, so a later order can bind it to a durable registry without changing this plane's semantics.
- **The `freshness-content-mismatch` reason code is collision-hardening**: a cache fact whose key matches the current derivation but whose semantics digest differs would require a sha256 collision over content that includes the semantics digest (the structural validation rejects drifted facts earlier — the observable drift paths are the validation rejection and `freshness-no-fact`). The check is retained as defense-in-depth, honestly documented as such.
- **Context-cost measurement is per-composition, not per-tokenizer**: token counts are attributed inputs (basis `observed`/`estimated` per segment); the plane measures compositions, it does not tokenize text (tokenizer integration is owned elsewhere and is not in this order's surfaces).
- **Joiner counts are per-coalesce-group role evidence**: the leader and joiners of one group read the settled count of THAT group; per-execution accounting rows differ by execution identity (the N=8 convergence test's per-execution row), which is the honest unit of accounting for coalesced groups.
