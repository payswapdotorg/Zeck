The work order forbids `platform/**`, `shared/**` and every other application's directory — the slice is strictly additive inside `benchmarks/validation/apps/economic-controls-direct/**`, mirroring session B's control-arm layout (VAL-043's competing arm as the direct template). The app rides the public boundary through the `ValidationHarness` seam and imports (never modifies) the VAL-040 substrate: `../economic-baseline/protocol` (the fixed-quality / fixed-cost arm terms with their statistical minimums), `../economic-baseline/normalization` (the micro-USD discipline, per-resolved-outcome cost, the NULL zero-resolved discipline), `../economic-baseline/pricing` (the pinned list-price manifest revisions, the FX table, `deriveManifestIntegrity`), and `verifyEconomicAppContract` from `../economic-baseline/driver`.

**The direct-arm oracles (all in the app-local driver):** `deriveDirectPathConformance` (the provider dispatch carries NO platform/gateway artifacts — no reuse/cache shortcut signatures, no platform mediation markers; a shortcut-riding dispatch FAILs mechanically with both artifacts named), `deriveDirectCostBasisIntegrity` (every cost fact is a MEASURED usage fact in a single pinned currency, normalized through the manifest's FX rail — a mixed-currency conflation or an estimate-backed cost FAILs named), `deriveDirectManifestIntegrity` (every price resolves from a PINNED manifest revision — an unpinned or mutated revision FAILs by digest disagreement), `deriveDirectSampleDiscipline` (the pre-registered corpus slice carries its minimum sample size — a starved sample FAILs; a post-hoc arm exclusion FAILs; the honest budget-stop PREFIX is the honest stop, never an exclusion). Per-attempt usage facts are the direct arm's granularity (retry overhead in scope on failed attempts — versus the competing arm's gateway aggregate). Wilson 95% intervals carried on every comparison.

The app's corpus declares, per row: the frozen portfolio reference (VAL-030 baseline revisions BY CONTENT DIGEST, never copied), the arm manifest entry (direct-provider kind, pinned price revision, fixed-quality or fixed-cost arm, statistical minimums), the task classes, and the expected normalized outcome. 7 honest controls (the USD default rail, the EUR per-1M rail, the JPY per-1K rail, the rev-002 corrected price, fixed-cost within budget, the honest budget-stop PREFIX 3-of-8, the zero-resolved NULL discipline) + 5 adversarial probes pinned as recorded data (platform-shortcut masquerade, mixed-currency conflation, estimate-backed cost, post-hoc arm exclusion, sample-size violation) + 2 env-gated live rows (honest NOT RUN).
