- **IDENTITY-IDEMPOTENCY** — `variantIrId`/`variantPlanId` are content-addressed over canonical forms; the identity variant's plan form is byte-identical to the IR's (variantPlanId IS the governed planId — the faithful-container proof, unit test); re-compiling the same input is a bounded no-op (identical output, trace and records — unit + integration proofs); re-appending the identical record replays (`replayed: true`), including under concurrency: N=8 simultaneous compile+append chains over real PG converge to exactly one durable row (the pure compiler produces the identical decisionId in every chain; the store's unique-index race safety serializes the appends — integration proof); content drift under a claimed identity remains a typed conflict (WORK-049's store contract, unchanged).
- **EXECUTION-PROVENANCE** — the exact chain plan → IR → variant → decision: every variant carries `sourcePlanId`/`sourceIrId` preserved verbatim plus the digest-chained pass trace (`passTraceDigest` in provenance); the trace records every pass application with per-site typed rejections; the whole compilation is replayable by deterministic re-compilation (byte-identical — unit + integration); the durable record references the base IR identity and the candidates reference the exact output variant; `auditDurableExecutionProvenance` audits the compiler-produced record with ZERO violations over the real store (integration).
- **VERIFICATION-SEPARATION** — verification anchors are carried VERBATIM, never interpreted: anchor materialization moves a terminal step-level strategy into an explicit verify step preserving the (anchored position, strategy) binding exactly (the core's anchor list is the invariant — unit proof); a mutation that invents a strategy is rejected (D10); the observability contract treats pure unanchored terminals as non-observable ONLY under the governing verification-anchor binding (the frozen completion binding carried by the constraints — the compiler never redefines the gate); the core treats verify steps as opaque observation points (strategies verbatim, strategy-less verifies as verbatim observation positions).

## Known limitations (honest boundaries)

- **Live model/substrate/provider selection: NOT RUN** — out of scope for stage 2 (and for this worker environment, which holds no provider credentials). The representation-ladder claims in the tests are explicit-basis fixtures (the `observed`/`estimated`/`defaulted` attribution contract is exactly the seam later E1.1 stages wire to real telemetry); the ladder hooks this order ships are the recorded decision points, never live selection.
- **Annotation-hook consumers are later stages**: memoization keys (WORK-052 cache planning), parallel/batch groups (runtime execution), subgraph decomposition (WORK-053/054 selection), result shapes (compact result allocation) are recorded decisions the later stages consume; nothing in this order executes work concurrently, caches results or batches calls.
- **Constant folding is closed-universe only**: steps fold iff their class is pure-deterministic, unbound (no capability/route/strategy) and their config is an EXACT closed static expression over the eight-operation evaluator universe inside the bounded size; anything the evaluator does not fully understand is never folded (typed per-site rejection). Folding of capability-bound or data-dependent computations is later work (it requires the executor-side semantics contracts).
- **Verification insertion is terminal-anchor materialization**: the provable form (a terminal step-level strategy becomes an explicit verify sink carrying the strategy verbatim); non-terminal anchored steps are conservatively rejected (re-binding them would change downstream dataflow). Invented strategies are impossible (D10).
- **Dead-step elimination is constraint-conditioned**: pure unanchored terminal chains are eliminable only while the governing constraints carry the verification-anchor completion binding (the frozen completion semantics); without it, the pass is the conservative no-op (all terminal outputs observable). A would-empty elimination is rejected inadmissible.
- **Retry normalization is the recorded-surface form**: canonical bounded parameters projected verbatim as annotations; structural retry rewriting is out of scope (opaque executor semantics — the later failure/recovery stage owns it). Unbounded retry configs are flagged (typed rejection), never silently blessed or rewritten.
