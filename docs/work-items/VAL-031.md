## Architecture note (within the work order's allowed surfaces)

`src/**` and `spec/**` are untouched (forbidden by this work order). The replay-analysis platform slice lives in `benchmarks/validation/platform/workload-replay.ts` (an allowed surface), mirroring the VAL-030/026 platform-slice pattern. The slice's PURE derivations: `derivePopulationCompleteness` (exactly N replays per row — a duplicated replay identity FAILS, a dropped replay FAILS, a gapped population FAILS), `deriveTrajectoryClassMembership` (each replay's digest must be a member of its row's VAL-030 pinned class — an out-of-class trajectory FAILS), `deriveStabilityHonesty` (the pre-learning fact every later learning claim must account for: deterministic rows show identical digests across their N replays; a legitimately-varying row is REPORTED varying with its observed distribution — a smoothed variance or a fabricated determinism claim FAILS mechanically), `deriveAccountingHonesty` (the VAL-006 discipline over per-replay totals — measured facts only), `deriveLatencyDistribution` (min/median/max measured, never estimated), the FNV-1a digest discipline (payload-free), the corpus-row oracle contract, `verifyWorkloadReplayAppContract`, and the replay-driver seam (one immutable trajectory identity per replay per the VAL-007/030 ledger discipline).

**The replay honesty invariants this slice pins:** a population is exactly N — duplicates and drops both FAIL mechanically; every replay's trajectory is class-checked against the frozen baseline (the VAL-030 registry is read-only — replays never rewrite the manifests); variance is honest — the varying row's two-digest distribution (4631d3db / f2d56105 at 2/2) is the observed fact, never smoothed; accounting totals are per-replay measured; latency is a measured distribution; evidence carries payload DIGESTS, never payload bytes.

The app (`benchmarks/validation/apps/workload-replay/`) declares the corpus (AC1): 10 offline rows + 1 env-gated live row. Per row the corpus declares the pinned baseline manifest entry (VAL-030's registry vocabulary), the replay count N (3–5), and the expected population statistics. The corpus includes the four deterministic replay populations, the legitimately-varying row, the guard-row replay population (FAILED honestly, zero dispatches), and five contamination-probe rows (duplicate/dropped/drifted/partial/fabricated-determinism shapes) whose honest controls prove the population oracles. The app rides the public SDK boundary (`createZeckClient` — no `src/**` import), fires every row's N replays through the harness's submit seam, reads the population back, and verifies its own contract with the PURE `verifyWorkloadReplayAppContract`.
