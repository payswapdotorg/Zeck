## Architecture note (within the work order's allowed surfaces)

`src/**`, `spec/**`, `benchmarks/validation/platform/**` and `benchmarks/validation/apps/shared/**` are untouched (forbidden by this work order while the longitudinal wave is in flight on those surfaces) — verified mechanically: `git diff --name-status <base>` shows ZERO modifications, only NEW files, all under the allowed surfaces. The slice is an **app-slice** (the economics wave's shape): everything lives in `benchmarks/validation/apps/economic-baseline/**`, with the pricing oracle, the platform-path executor and the accounting rails as INJECTED seams that IMPORT the existing modules (never copied, never modified).

**The normalization core** (`normalization.ts`): every arm's heterogeneous pricing — per-token list prices in mixed currencies (USD/EUR/JPY) and units (per-1M/per-1K/per-token), metered vs. batched metering (ceil-to-batch), retry/failure amortization (retry-overhead scoped facts of the same run) — converges onto the canonical basis (micro-USD cost per SUCCESSFULLY resolved outcome) through exact BigInt rational arithmetic with one deterministic half-up rounding per fact. A usage fact whose currency mismatches the pinned price's denomination, or sits outside the pinned FX table, FAILS to normalize (the mixed-currency conflation catch); measured and estimate facts are summed SEPARATELY and never conflated; cost-per-resolved is NULL when nothing resolved (never zero) and an estimate-backed number is REFUSED mechanically (the VAL-006 discipline extended to the cross-arm basis).

**The pinned price manifest** (`pricing.ts`): content-addressed and append-only — each revision's digest is the SHA-256 over the canonical JSON of its public list-price tables + its FX conversion table; a price correction is a NEW revision (`rev-002` supersedes `rev-001` — the openrouter input-price correction; `rev-001` stays frozen and readable); an in-place price mutation breaks digest agreement mechanically; negotiated/bulk/undocumented pricing is FORBIDDEN (the table validator rejects any non-public-list source). Prices are NEVER copied inline into rows or arm declarations — arms declare WHICH revision priced their runs, and the pricing oracle resolves prices through the registry only.

**The frozen experiment protocol** (`protocol.ts`): the arm-declaration grammar formalizes the VAL-005 comparator vocabulary for the economics wave — fixed-quality arms pin a declared resolution threshold (the evaluation oracle's resolution discipline) and measure the cost to attain it over the FULL pre-registered slice; fixed-cost arms pin a micro-USD budget and measure the quality attained within it over the pre-registered order (the budget stop is a declared PREFIX stop, never a post-hoc exclusion); both declare the statistical minimum sample size (enforced mechanically) and the pinned price revision; the declaration is digest-frozen at pin time (drifted terms fail `verifyDeclarationDrift` mechanically); every comparison carries the Wilson 95% interval IMPORTED from the REAL accounting module (never re-implemented) — a confidence-less comparison FAILS the validation battery; threshold attainment is RECOMPUTED from the accounted runs (an arm's own claim never decides — the threshold-gaming catch); budget respect is judged on the MEASURED total only.

**The execution driver** (`driver.ts`): drives one experiment row through the platform path — the landed execution's chain runs the ARM DECISION BEFORE DISPATCH (the fixed-cost arm's per-round worst-case bound, priced from the pinned manifest, recorded in the durable planning decision BEFORE the first dispatch; the budget gate never dispatches a round the pinned budget cannot cover), each round dispatches through the INJECTED executor (the recorded-replay executor offline; the REAL model gateway live) with bounded retry on RETRYABLE categories only, every attempt's usage converges through the normalization core onto canonical micro-USD, each round is sealed + evaluated + aggregated through the INJECTED accounting rails (`createRealAccountingRails()` binds the REAL `ValidationRecorder`, the REAL `evaluateRun` and the REAL `aggregateArm` — imported from their existing modules), and the comparison is derived + validated against the frozen protocol. The verdict is MECHANICAL: any failure, any failed criterion, any comparison violation → verdict fail → terminal FAILED (never a partial-success shortcut); the observed terminal is read back from the ledger and a disagreement with the derived verdict fails the row honestly (the anyFail→FAILED probe at the ledger).

**The app** (`application.ts`): the customer-style application riding the public SDK boundary — one pinned corpus row per run (the submission → the completion poll → the result retrieval → the deterministic assertions), the task body carrying REFERENCES ONLY (the arm identity, the pinned price revision, the slice shape — never an inline price), and the app-side economic contract (the terminal↔criteria agreement over the public result read — a fabricated pass-with-fail FAILs the app honestly).

The corpus declares 10 rows: 8 offline (deterministic) + 2 live (env-gated). The discrimination battery's controlled fakes live in `fixtures.ts` (the leaky replay-executor knobs: `conflateCurrency`, `estimateOnly`, `dropFailedRounds`, `truncateSamples`, the mutated-manifest builder with a stale digest, the honest corrected-revision builder, the fabricating lifecycle, the fake ledger/seam/API world).
