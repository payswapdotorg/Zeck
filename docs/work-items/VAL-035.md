## Architecture note (within the work order's allowed surfaces)

`src/**` and `spec/**` are untouched (forbidden by this work order). The canary platform slice lives in `benchmarks/validation/platform/canary-promotion.ts` (an allowed surface), mirroring the VAL-030..034 platform-slice pattern. The slice's PURE derivations: `deriveCanaryPolicyExplicitness` (every executed ramp step's decision cites its policy — the ramp schedule, the failure-budget and the divergence tolerance stated AND checked; an unstated policy or a stated-but-unchecked item FAILs with the item named), `derivePromotionLifecycleCompleteness` (the candidate's walk must be the FULL evidenced chain through `shadow-executed` before the canary — a skipped stage, a broken walk or an unevidenced transition FAILs; the canary advances `shadow-executed → canaried → promoted` one evidenced rung at a time, a jump FAILs), `deriveBreachHonesty` (an observed divergence count beyond the pinned budget FAILs the step and the decision MUST be `breach-rollback` — a smoothed breach that advances FAILs, and a breach decision without its rollback trigger FAILs), `deriveRollbackCompleteness` (the rollback is COMPLETE and mechanical — the served traffic reverts to the incumbent across the whole slice; a partial rollback that leaves any residual fraction serving the replacement FAILs naming the residual; an unexercised rollback plan FAILs), `deriveSliceIsolation` (an unpromoted candidate serves ONLY its canary slice — an over-slice serve FAILs, an out-of-slice tenant FAILs, an under-serve that breaks the pinned ramp FAILs; after promotion the full traffic serves the replacement), `deriveCanaryCostSeparation` (canary-slice cost measured apart under its `canary` marker — an unmarked canary cost billed as ordinary served traffic FAILs), the mid-canary containment carry-over (an escape during canary execution is a named containment violation that FAILs), the append-only decision/divergence/rollback/cost ledgers, the FNV-1a digest discipline (payload-free), `deriveCanaryRowCriteria` (the per-row mechanical verdict), and `verifyCanaryPromotionAppContract` (the app-boundary oracle).

**The promotion honesty invariants this slice pins:** every canary decision is policy-governed (stated AND checked — never an unchecked budget); a budget breach rolls back COMPLETELY (exercised, never partial — the reversibility contract of the deterministicization contract is PROVEN by exercising the rollback, not asserted); an unpromoted candidate never serves beyond its slice (tenant boundaries included); the canary cost is measured apart under its marker (the customer is never billed for the canary); the lifecycle advances one evidenced rung (`canaried`, then `promoted` on a clean full ramp) and NEVER by jumps; the VAL-032..034 registries and lifecycle history are read-only input (a different-content commit under a recorded identity THROWS); evidence carries payload DIGESTS, never payload bytes.

The app (`benchmarks/validation/apps/canary-promotion/`) declares the corpus (AC1): 12 offline rows + 1 env-gated live row. Per row the corpus declares the canaried candidate (a VAL-034 shadow-executed lifecycle identity with its prior evidenced walk), the pinned policy (the ramp schedule, the failure-budget, the divergence tolerance), the traffic population and the expected outcome. The corpus includes the four clean-promotion rows (one per candidate kind — the full ramp 5% → 25% → 50% → 100% advancing to `promoted`), the budget-breach-rollback row (the removed-call replacement breaches its budget at the first ramp step — FAILED honestly, the rollback exercised and complete), the two honest refusals (unregistered candidate; not-yet-shadow-executed candidate), and six canary-probe rows (skipped-lifecycle / unchecked-policy / smoothed-breach / over-slice / partial-rollback / mid-canary-escape) whose populations and policies are pinned now and whose adversarial fake-path variants FAIL mechanically in the discrimination suites. The app rides the public SDK boundary (`createZeckClient` — no `src/**` import), drives each row's canary run through the harness's submit seam, reads the decisions/rollback/cost/lifecycle landing back (RE-DERIVING the contract, never trusting the platform's claim), and verifies its own contract with the PURE `verifyCanaryPromotionAppContract`.
