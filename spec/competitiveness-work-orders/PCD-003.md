## Objective

Deliver the P4 program's evaluation: the shadow/offline evaluation app (ACR-011 §4) that measures whether a scorer configuration earns promotion — calibration, top-k decision quality, worst-case regressions and policy-constraint preservation, with the scorer's own cost and latency NETTED against lift — under a mechanical promotion gate where claims alone promote nothing. This is the roadmap's decision-sequence item: "evaluate optional learned/System One scoring in shadow/offline mode" before any production claim.

## Required deliverables

1. **The evaluation app** (`benchmarks/validation/apps/` — the PCL-003 `latency-tail-evaluation` placement precedent, TRUE work-order tag): per-score-kind calibration (predicted vs observed with honest binning, never a single blended number), top-k decision quality (scored vs deterministic-baseline orderings on the compiler's actual selection outcomes), worst-case regression tracking per latency/cost class (the ACR-010 percentile honesty applied to scorer-influenced selections), policy-constraint preservation verification (scored selections never violate policy/budget/verification constraints), and scorer cost truth (invocation cost + latency measured and netted against lift).
2. **The declared-before thresholds** (`experiments/pcd-003/declared-thresholds.json`, committed at the skeleton milestone — the PCL-003 discipline): the promotion gate's four parts declared before any results exist — (net lift on the primary decision-quality metric) AND (no calibration regression beyond declared delta) AND (no worst-case regression beyond declared delta) AND (policy-constraint preservation proven).
3. **The mechanical promotion gate**: the 4-part AND evaluated only from measured evidence; a miss with honest attribution is a valid delivery (the truth discipline); canary/shadow modes only — the gate emits a PROMOTED/NOT-PROMOTED verdict with the full evidence table; production enablement remains a separate operator-era decision, never implied by the gate.
4. **The honest replay corpus**: re-run the deterministic-baseline arm over the existing evaluation corpora (the PPR-027/PPR-028 application-compatibility cells where latency and outcome truth already exist); quote recorded results verbatim, never relabel; fresh typed-facts re-runs where the PCD-001/PCD-002 planes apply; synthetic-supply/synthetic-cost labels preserved (the PPR-027 discipline).
5. **Validation suites**: engine oracles (gate arithmetic, binning, netting), mirror pins against the REAL PCD-001/PCD-002 planes (mirror-by-value, pinned in-suite — the PCS-004 precedent), and committed-evidence verification (digests byte-identical across re-runs; wall-clock and run identities excluded from every digest projection).
6. **The full existing battery stays green** (see Acceptance criteria).
