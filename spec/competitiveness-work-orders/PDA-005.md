## Objective

Deliver the roadmap §P5 investment 5: predictable tenant control — explicit quality/cost/latency/policy constraints and observable why-not explanations, WITHOUT exposing internal provider-specific semantics as application contract. A tenant that asks for something and does not get it must be able to ask WHY and receive an honest, typed, contract-level answer (constraint X at value Y; policy Z), never a provider-internal leak and never a silent gap.

## Required deliverables (binding shape; the ACR is normative)

1. The ACR (its own PR, the ACR-008..011 precedent): the why-not explanation surface typed as contract (closed vocabulary over the existing constraint/policy/admission records; content-addressed; absence-equivalent).
2. The explanation seam: explanations derived from the SAME records that made the decision (never a parallel narrative; the decision record is the explanation's only source).
3. The no-provider-leak proof: a pinned architecture test that the explanation vocabulary carries no provider-internal identifiers (the contract-stability discipline).
4. Validation suites per the ACR + the full existing battery green.

## Non-goals / forbidden surfaces

- NO new decision authority: explanations OBSERVE decisions, they never make or override them.
- NO provider-internal semantics in the application contract.
- Workers cannot merge their own PRs; the Lead reviews, runs the independent battery, and merges.
