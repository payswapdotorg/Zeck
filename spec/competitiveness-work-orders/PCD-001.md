# PCD-001 — Decision-Score Typed Facts: The Prediction Plane

Status: AUTHORIZED (first work order of the P4 program)
Program: zeck-competitiveness-p4
Depends: ACR-011 §2 (normative)
Preferred concurrency: 1 worker
Current governing architecture: v1.0 + D1.0 + E1.0 + E1.1 + ACR-006 + ACR-007 + ACR-008 + ACR-009 + ACR-010 + ACR-011

## Objective

Deliver the P4 program's prediction plane: the `DecisionScoreFacts` typed artifact (ACR-011 §2) — the closed, optional, prediction-only score fact block that the scorer seam (PCD-002) will produce and the evaluation (PCD-003) will consume read-only. A score is a prediction with provenance and bounds, never a measurement and never an authority; this work order builds the contract that makes an interchangeable scorer honest by construction.

## Required deliverables

1. **The typed artifact** (`src/modules/planning/domain/` at the plane's real home — study the PCL-001 `outcome-latency-facts.ts` and PCS-002 `semantic-fidelity.ts` placement precedents and the module layering per AGENTS.md):
   - the closed vocabularies: score kind (candidate-success | failure-class-distribution | verifier-value | route-suitability), scorer kind (deterministic-baseline | external-scorer | zeck-trained), confidence discipline (bounded | unbounded-unknown);
   - typed bounded values: probability in [0,1] for candidate-success; a mass over the ACR-010 per-leg failure taxonomy summing to ≤ 1 with the honest remainder for failure-class-distribution; non-negative bounded estimates with declared scales for verifier-value and route-suitability;
   - explicit provenance on every fact (scorer identity + version + configuration digest, calibration basis digest, candidate identity, scoring context digest);
   - pure boundary checking: both-sides fail-closed pre-validation, absence skips with typed reason, mismatches typed naming both sides;
   - determinism + content addressing: canonical tagged-tuple JSON + injected digest, no env/clock/randomness in the fact derivation;
   - absence equivalence: undefined and null both the absent spelling;
   - the prediction/measurement separation law: no import of or digest-namespace sharing with the outcome-evidence planes (outcome-latency-facts and kin) — pinned by an architecture test.
2. **Tests**: unit + discrimination suites covering every closed-vocabulary member, every fail-closed boundary, the provenance incoherence rejections, the mass-summing honesty for failure-class-distribution, the digest determinism, and the absence-equivalence discipline; architecture tests pinning the module's placement, the no-new-durable-surface law and the prediction/measurement separation.
3. **The full existing battery stays green** (see Acceptance criteria).
