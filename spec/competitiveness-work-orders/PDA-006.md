## Objective

Deliver the roadmap §P5 investment 6 and the decision sequence's final item: the REAL developer-adoption cohort — recruit actual developer teams for counterbalanced direct-baseline and Zeck-exposure periods using comparable tasks; measure migration time, operational burden, durable use and final preference; report confidence and attrition; provide evidence that could FALSIFY the preference hypothesis. The roadmap's guardrail is binding: scenario-simulation percentages are NEVER reused as adoption evidence.

## Required deliverables

1. **The experiment protocol** (`docs/` — e.g. `docs/ADOPTION-EXPERIMENT-PROTOCOL.md`): the counterbalanced design (period order, washout, task comparability), the measured variables (time-to-first-certified-task via the PDA-001 preflight, app-side changed lines, operational-burden instrument, durable-use telemetry with consent boundaries, final preference instrument), the attrition/confidence reporting discipline, and the falsifiability statement — what result would DISCONFIRM the lower-friction hypothesis.
2. **The measurement harness** (`experiments/pda-006/` tooling per the repo's experiment patterns): cohort bookkeeping, period counterbalancing, instrument delivery, and the honest-evidence store — every measurement recorded with provenance; the analysis emits evidence tables, never marketing copy.
3. **The operator boundary, explicit**: RECRUITMENT IS OPERATOR-ERA. The Lead builds the instrument; the operator recruits the teams. Until real cohorts exist, every adoption-evidence field renders NOT RUN (owner: operator) — never backfilled, never simulated.
4. **Validation suites**: harness oracles (counterbalancing correctness, instrument delivery idempotence), committed-evidence verification (digests byte-identical; wall-clock excluded).
5. **The full existing battery stays green** (see PDA-001 acceptance standard).

## Non-goals / forbidden surfaces

- NO cohort fabrication, NO synthetic adoption numbers, NO reuse of scenario-simulation percentages as adoption evidence.
- NO changes under `src/**`, `sdk/**`, `compat/**`, `deploy/**`.
- NO market-preference claims from anything less than the real cohort's recorded evidence.
- Workers cannot merge their own PRs; the Lead reviews, runs the independent battery, and merges.
