# PCS-003 — Planner Quality-Contract Gating and Substitute Disclosure (the Wiring)

Status: AUTHORIZED (dependency-gated on PCS-002)
Program: zeck-competitiveness-p2
Depends: PCS-002 (the typed facts) + ACR-009 §3–§5 (normative)
Preferred concurrency: 1 worker
Current governing architecture: v1.0 + D1.0 + E1.0 + E1.1 + ACR-006 + ACR-007 + ACR-008 + ACR-009

## Objective

Wire the semantic-fidelity facts into the EXISTING planner selection and the public disclosure surface, exactly as ACR-009 §3–§5 specify: a task that declares a semantic quality requirement makes a `deterministic-substitute` representation inadmissible with a typed reason; a task that permits substitutes may select them with the substitution recorded; the user-facing projection shows which requirement is met and what remains unavailable. The wiring is default-off/absence-equivalent: every existing call site, test and evidence record is byte-identical until a task authors a quality contract or a capability declares fidelity facts.

## Required deliverables

1. **Planner gating** (in the existing planning/selection machinery — consume PCS-002's facts read-only; NO second planner):
   - the admissibility evaluation gains the typed quality-contract dimension: declared `requires-semantic-retrieval-quality` (or the task-authored equivalent in the closed vocabulary) + `deterministic-substitute` representation → INADMISSIBLE with a typed reason (composed exactly like the existing inadmissibility reasons — the economics/quality/policy reason family);
   - no quality contract declared (or substitutes explicitly permitted) → the substitute remains admissible AND the selection records the substitution fact on the decision record (the existing WORK-049 decision-record seam — append-only values, no new durable surface);
   - fail-closed on unknown/malformed quality contracts (inadmissible, never permissive);
   - the gating NEVER lowers a quality floor, raises a budget ceiling or bypasses policy — it is a new typed admissibility dimension over the existing machinery (invariant 13 boundary).

2. **User-facing disclosure** (the existing public evidence/projection surface):
   - where a task's declared requirement is met by a substitute, or is unmet and blocked, the projection shows: which requirement was declared, which representation satisfied it (kind + provenance), and what remains unavailable — mechanically derived from the typed facts, never prose-only;
   - the honest NOT-RUN/unmet boundary discipline the records already carry is extended with the typed facts so the disclosure is machine-checkable;
   - the disclosure is projected only where facts exist (absence-equivalent: no facts → the projection is byte-identical to today).

3. **Absence-equivalence proof**:
   - on the real wired stack, the absence of fidelity facts and quality contracts selects and records byte-identically to the pre-change baseline (the PCR-002 off-behavior proof pattern: the full traced decision table over the real stack, byte-compared);
   - discrimination tests: gating on/off via the declared contract only (no ambient flag), the typed inadmissibility reason, the substitution-record-on-permitted-selection path, the fail-closed malformed-contract path.

4. **Tests** (the established discipline):
   - unit + discrimination + architecture suites covering every new path;
   - architecture tests proving no second planner, no ambient configuration, no new durable surface;
   - the full existing battery stays green.
