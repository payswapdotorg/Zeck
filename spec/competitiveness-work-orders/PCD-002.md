## Objective

Deliver the P4 program's wiring: the single scorer-reading seam (ACR-011 §3) — a configuration-driven adapter boundary where an interchangeable scorer (external or zeck-trained) produces `DecisionScoreFacts` for the compiler's admissible candidates — plus the declarative scorer configuration, default-off and absence-equivalent, with the ordering-only influence law and the scorer-cost measurement that PCD-003's promotion gate consumes.

## Required deliverables

1. **The declarative scorer configuration** (`src/modules/planning/domain/` — the PCL-002 `tail-pacing-configuration.ts` pattern: typed, closed, versioned configuration DATA, never an engine): scorer kind, score kinds authorized, declared bounds, declared timeout, the injection binding for the scorer function, version — with the fail-closed constructor rejecting undeclared score kinds, missing bounds, unbounded timeouts.
2. **The single seam** (`src/modules/planning/adapters/` — the PCL-002 `tail-pacing.ts` pattern): absent configuration ⇒ the inert resolver returning absence verdicts byte-identical to today's (proven over the real wired stack with a sha256-pinned baseline); present-but-malformed ⇒ typed `ScorerConfigurationError` at construction; present-and-valid ⇒ invokes the composition-injected scorer under the declared timeout and returns `DecisionScoreFacts` or a typed failure.
3. **The ordering-only influence law** (the wiring discipline): scores are read AFTER the hard policy admissibility filter and may only refine ordering among already-admissible candidates; a scored selection can never widen, bypass or override a prohibition; the deterministic-first preference stays untouchable (the LRN-002 `learned-policy-consultation.ts` discipline, verbatim). Scorer latency and invocation cost are captured as first-class measured facts on the consultation record (PCD-003's net-lift input).
4. **Tests**: unit suites for the configuration fail-closed boundaries and the seam contract; discrimination suites D-pinned (absent vs armed configurations differing exactly on the declared dimensions — the deterministic mechanics tables pinned by digest); architecture tests pinning the single-seam law (no second scorer entry point), the no-ambient-configuration law, and the advisory-only/ordering-only consumption law; the absence-equivalence proof over the real wired stack (sha256-pinned baseline, byte-identical at HEAD with no configuration authored).
5. **The full existing battery stays green** (see Acceptance criteria).
