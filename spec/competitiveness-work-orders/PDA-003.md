## Objective

Deliver the roadmap §P5 investment 3: the evidence-backed ROI report — compare per-success cost, reliability, tail latency, avoided infrastructure (provider routing, retry/fallback, credential management, evidence plumbing) and portability against the direct and strong-optimized arms. Every number comes from RECORDED results at exact revisions; synthetic, inferred and NOT RUN states are labeled per claim. This is the report the roadmap's honesty guardrails demand before any developer-facing value claim.

## Required deliverables

1. **The ROI report** (`docs/` — e.g. `docs/DEVELOPER-ROI-REPORT.md`): the comparison table set (per-success cost incl. duplicates, resolution/reliability, p50/p95/p99 tails per failure class, avoided-infrastructure inventory, portability) — Zeck-mediated vs direct vs strong-optimized — sourced exclusively from the recorded evidence: the PPR-027/PPR-028 application-compatibility cells, `deploy/evidence/*.json`, the P3 promotion-gate results (PCL-003), the capstone and compatibility observed-evidence documents.
2. **The evidence index** (`experiments/pda-003/evidence-index.json`): a machine-readable mapping of every report claim → its source artifact, revision and digest; the report is regenerable from the index.
3. **The labeling law**: every claim carries its evidence class (recorded-live / recorded-synthetic / inferred / NOT RUN with owner); no projected numbers; confidence stated honestly; the report's headline never outruns its weakest recorded evidence.
4. **Validation suite**: committed-evidence verification (digests byte-identical across re-runs; wall-clock and run identities excluded from every digest projection; an index entry pointing at a missing artifact fails the suite).
5. **The full existing battery stays green** (see PDA-001 acceptance standard).

## Non-goals / forbidden surfaces

- NO new measurements: this work order REPORTS recorded evidence; running new arms is out of scope (any gap is a labeled NOT RUN with its owner).
- NO changes under `src/**`, `sdk/**`, `examples/**`, `compat/**`, `deploy/**` (evidence artifacts are frozen input).
- NO adoption or preference claims (PDA-006's territory; the roadmap's guardrail verbatim: do not claim Zeck universally beats strong in-house engineering — the observed evidence does not support that).
- Workers cannot merge their own PRs; the Lead reviews, runs the independent battery, and merges.
