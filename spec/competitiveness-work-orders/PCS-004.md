## Objective

Deliver the P2 program's evidence: an evaluation proving the semantic-fidelity gating does what ACR-009 §6 requires — quality fixtures where model-backed semantic quality is measurably distinct from deterministic substitutes, negative controls proving a task requiring semantic retrieval FAILS CLOSED on a deterministic substitute, mechanically differentiated evidence, and the honest replay of the PPR-025/PPR-026 embeddings lines under the gated configuration.

## Required deliverables

1. **Evaluation application** (a new `benchmarks/validation/apps/` app in the established pattern — study `economic-recovery-plan-evaluation` first; carry your TRUE work-order tag `PCS-004` on the README first line, resolving through the PCS-001 vocabulary extension; consume the seam helpers through the lab's shared re-export surface):
   - **quality fixtures**: curated semantic-retrieval fixtures where model-backed semantic quality is measurably distinct from deterministic lexical/hash substitutes (e.g. paraphrase/synonym retrieval, cross-lingual nearest-neighbor, polysemy discrimination) — the fixture corpus is committed, digest-pinned and synthetic-supply labeled;
   - **negative controls** (the hard gate): tasks declaring `requires-semantic-retrieval-quality` offered ONLY a deterministic substitute must FAIL CLOSED with the typed inadmissibility reason — **a task requiring semantic retrieval must not pass merely because a deterministic vector is produced**; every negative control's failure mode is asserted mechanically;
   - **differentiated evidence**: a consumer reading only the typed facts (no prose) separates `semantic-embeddings` from `deterministic-vectorization` on every emitted record — proven by an in-suite mechanical discriminator;
   - **replay of the PPR-025/PPR-026 lines**: the embeddings edges re-run under the gated configuration with honest per-threshold verdicts and explicit outcome thresholds declared up front; no relabeling of the recorded P2-FIX-era results.

2. **Threshold report** (in the `threshold-report.md` pattern of PCR-003):
   - explicit thresholds declared BEFORE the run, honest verdicts after (a threshold miss with attribution is a valid delivery — the truth discipline);
   - reproducibility: corpus digest + evaluation digest pinned, re-run digest comparison byte-identical (the deterministic replay discipline; wall-clock and run identities excluded from every digest projection);
   - honest boundaries: synthetic-supply/synthetic-cost/synthetic-latency labels on every number; live credentialed semantic-quality validation remains the operator-era obligation, never backfilled.

3. **Validation suites** (in the `tests/unit/validation/` pattern — `pcs-004-*.test.ts`): the evaluation's construction laws, fixture integrity, negative-control assertions and digest reproducibility all tested in-suite.
