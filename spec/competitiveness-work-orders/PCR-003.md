## Objective

Produce the P1 evidence: a fresh three-arm replay evaluation (un-retried direct / strong-optimized / mediated-with-plan) at ONE exact revision, targeting the multi-provider cells where the capstone measured the gap, with explicit outcome thresholds and honest verdicts per ACR-008 §7.

## Required deliverables

1. **Evaluation extension** (in the established benchmark app pattern — study `benchmarks/validation/apps/economic-competitive-benchmark/` and the controls apps first):
   - a mediated-with-plan arm configuration that turns plan mode ON (the PCR-002 evaluation configuration) with a bounded plan (the recovery ladder the strong baseline exercises: same-provider retry + alternate-provider failover, bounded by the same attempt economics discipline);
   - multi-provider cell emphasis: the cell family where mediated resolution measured 92.4% vs single-provider 97.2%;
   - per-attempt evidence: every attempt recorded with attribution, strategy verdict, measured cost, latency — reproducible by re-run (deterministic replay discipline);
   - the strong-optimized and un-retried-direct arms re-run FRESH at the same revision (the exact-revision rule — never reuse the capstone's recorded numbers).

2. **The threshold report** (a durable evidence document in the delivery):
   - resolution: mediated-with-plan vs strong-optimized in the targeted multi-provider cells — threshold: equal-or-better (≥ 98.0%-class parity with the fresh strong baseline);
   - failure-adjusted cost per resolved outcome: improved vs strong-optimized, or a documented tradeoff with attribution;
   - policy/budget violations: ZERO — a hard gate; any violation is a delivery-blocking finding;
   - latency tails: p95/p99 by failure class and route — explicit tradeoff accounting, no silent regressions;
   - every number carries its synthetic-supply/synthetic-cost label; a threshold miss with honest attribution is a VALID delivery (the program's truth discipline); no market/production claims.

3. **Tests**: the evaluation's own validation suites in the established pattern (config pinning, arm digest integrity, determinism/reproducibility, honest-label presence) — study val-048's suite shape first.

4. **The PCR-004 eligibility input**: an explicit statement whether sequential plans reached the resolution threshold on side-effect-safe idempotent cells (this gates the hedged-mode work order).
