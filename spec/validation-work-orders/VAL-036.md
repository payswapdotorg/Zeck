## Objective

Deliver the maturity slice: the mechanical engine that closes the
longitudinal determinization loop — the benchmark that measures, over
the recorded lifecycle history (VAL-032..035's registries, ledgers and
lifecycle records, read-only input), how the workload portfolio's
determinization MATURITY evolves across the promotion generations:
per-workload-generation model-call reduction (the promoted replacements
displacing incumbent AI rounds), the measured cost/latency deltas per
generation (the VAL-006 discipline over the REAL accounting rails),
the learning-curve analysis (which workload families determinize fast
— reaching stable promoted states in few generations — and which stay
variable across generations, reported honestly with their observed
distributions), and the maturity verdict per workload family (the
honest classification: determinized-stable / determinizing-trending /
variable-resilient / immature-insufficient-evidence).

The verification core is the maturity oracle: a maturity claim must
cite its evidence population (the exact lifecycle records, promotion
generations and accounting measurements it generalizes from — an
uncited or partially-cited claim FAILs); a learning-curve claim from a
gapped generation series (a missing generation, or a generation whose
measurements are absent) FAILs; a maturity classification that
contradicts the observed data (a variable-resilient family classified
determinized-stable, or a determinized family reported variable) FAILs
mechanically; and the analysis is honest about evidence limits — a
family with insufficient generations is classified
immature-insufficient-evidence, never stretched into a trend (a trend
claim from insufficient evidence FAILs).

The economics are the point of the benchmark: every generation's
reported savings must reconcile with the recorded accounting (the
incumbent's measured cost vs the replacement's measured cost per
displaced call — a savings number that does not reconcile with the
recorded ledgers FAILs); the savings attribution is per-mechanism
(reuse vs cache vs competence vs deterministicization displacements
attributed separately — an aggregate savings number without its
per-mechanism breakdown FAILs); and payload digests only, never
payload bytes.

## Acceptance criteria

1. The application rides the public SDK boundary with a
   repository-reproducible, secret-free configuration and executable
   tests; the corpus declares, per row, the workload family, the
   generation series (the recorded lifecycle + accounting evidence it
   analyzes) and the expected maturity classification + curve facts.
2. Deterministic fixtures make every offline row reproducible; every
   analysis cites its full evidence population with digests; the
   per-mechanism savings attribution reconciles with the recorded
   accounting ledgers.
3. A REAL end-to-end integration run (env-gated) drives the maturity
   corpus over the REAL platform path: the REAL lifecycle history and
   accounting records serve the analysis, the maturity verdicts and
   learning curves are recorded over REAL SQL, and the runs are
   measured by the REAL accounting rails.
4. Mechanical verification per row: evidence-citation completeness
   (full population, no gaps), curve-series integrity (gapless
   generations, recorded measurements only), classification fidelity
   (the verdict matches the observed data), savings reconciliation
   (per-mechanism, ledger-matched) — probed adversarially (an uncited
   claim, a gapped series, a misclassified family, an unreconciled
   savings number and an insufficient-evidence trend each FAIL
   mechanically).
5. Honest economics and latency (measured on the live rows,
   none-reported honestly offline); payload digests (never payloads)
   in evidence.
6. Discrimination tests prove the maturity analysis against controlled
   fakes (uncited claim, gapped series, misclassification,
   unreconciled savings, insufficient-evidence trend).
7. Evidence document records REAL outcomes, timings, costs, NOT RUN
   boundaries and the executed battery honestly.
