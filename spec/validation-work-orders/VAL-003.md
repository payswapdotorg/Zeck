# VAL-003 — Golden Task, Environment-State, Outcome and Safety Corpus

**Status:** PLANNED (becomes dispatchable after VAL-001)
**Program:** Zeck Validation Roadmap
**Assurance:** HIGH_ASSURANCE
**Dependencies:** VAL-001

## Objective

Create the first version of the reusable validation corpus: realistic customer tasks, controlled environment states, expected outcomes, forbidden outcomes, quality rubrics, safety constraints and reproducible fixtures spanning the supported Zeck workload families.

## Required workload coverage

The initial corpus must include representative scenarios for text, structured extraction/transformation, RAG, tool use, business workflows, long-running execution, voice/realtime voice, image generation/transformation, video/media generation, image recognition, VLM, audio understanding, multimodal work, 3D rendering, customer service, browser use, computer use, research, coding, operations and human-in-the-loop workflows.

Each application family should begin with approximately 20–50 meaningful tasks plus edge/failure/adversarial cases where applicable, then grow from observed failures.

## Allowed surfaces

- `benchmarks/validation/corpus/**`
- fixtures and evaluation data required by the corpus
- validation schemas/tests
- `docs/work-items/VAL-003.md`

## Forbidden

- production secrets or personal data;
- unverifiable expected outcomes presented as ground truth;
- provider-specific assumptions as architecture;
- changing product behavior solely to make the corpus pass.

## Acceptance criteria

1. A versioned schema represents task input, environment state, expected outcome, forbidden outcome, quality rubric, safety constraints, latency target and evaluation method.
2. Every required workload family has at least one executable scenario family.
3. Outcome assertions distinguish final response quality from actual environment/state effects.
4. Safety cases include authority-boundary and side-effect constraints where relevant.
5. Corpus fixtures are deterministic/reproducible or explicitly classify nondeterministic tolerances.
6. Corpus version and task identities are included in run metadata.
7. Data provenance and licensing/usage boundaries are recorded for any external corpus material.
8. The corpus can expand from observed production-like failures without invalidating historical runs.

## Evidence / issue protocol

For any ambiguous or flaky ground truth, record the ambiguity and a proposed resolution rather than silently changing expected results. Recommend oracle, evaluator or fixture improvements with trade-offs.
