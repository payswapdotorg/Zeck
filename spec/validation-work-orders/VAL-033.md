## Objective

Deliver the equivalence slice: the mechanical engine that takes the
discovery's PROPOSALS (VAL-032's candidate registry entries — reuse
shapes, cache transforms, competence patterns, deterministicization
candidates, each with its cited evidence population) and, for every
proposed deterministic replacement, GENERATES the replacement as
untrusted isolated code, drives the DIFFERENTIAL evaluation (the
deterministic candidate against the incumbent AI implementation over
the representative historical inputs — VAL-031's recorded replay
populations — plus the pinned adversarial cases), and records the
equivalence verdict with an EXPLICIT, mechanically verified acceptance
criterion per the deterministicization contract: equality is not
required for semantic outputs, but the acceptance criterion must be
stated and verified — a replacement accepted under an unstated
criterion, or one whose criterion is stated but never checked, FAILS.

The verification core is the equivalence oracle: a replacement whose
provenance chain is broken (no citation back to the VAL-032 proposal
and its evidence population) FAILS; an equivalence verdict drawn from
a PARTIAL differential population (the historical inputs without the
adversarial cases, or a subset of the cited replay population) FAILS;
a differential case where the replacement diverges from its stated
acceptance criterion FAILS (the divergence is recorded honestly, never
smoothed); and the lifecycle discipline holds — an equivalence-verified
replacement advances to the equivalence-verified stage ONLY (shadow,
canary and promotion are VAL-034/035's scope — a skipped-stage
promotion FAILS mechanically).

The generation is ISOLATED per the contract's safety rule: synthesized
programs and tools are treated as untrusted code until isolated
validation succeeds — a replacement that attempts to escape its
isolation boundary (touching platform state, the network, credentials
or tenant boundaries beyond its granted surface) FAILS and is recorded
as a containment violation, never silently forgiven.

## Forbidden

- `src/**` and `spec/**` changes;
- applications importing `src/**`;
- mutating any frozen baseline artifact, recorded trajectory or the
  candidate registry's EXISTING entries (VAL-030/031/032's registries
  and ledgers are read-only inputs; the equivalence verdict APPENDS to
  the lifecycle, it never rewrites a proposal);
- PROMOTING any replacement past the equivalence-verified stage
  (shadow/canary/promotion are VAL-034/035's scope — a skipped-stage
  promotion FAILS);
- executing synthesized replacement code outside its isolation
  boundary (untrusted code discipline — a containment escape FAILS);
- credentials in the repository, logs or reports;
- fabricated model results or fabricated differential outcomes;
- recording payload bytes in evidence (digest references only).

## Acceptance criteria

1. The application rides the public SDK boundary with a
   repository-reproducible, secret-free configuration and executable
   tests; the corpus declares, per row, the source proposal (the
   VAL-032 registry identity it tests), the replacement shape (the
   synthesized deterministic program/tool with its granted isolation
   surface), the differential population (the cited historical replay
   inputs + the pinned adversarial cases) and the expected equivalence
   verdict (or the honest divergence/containment failure).
2. Deterministic fixtures make every offline row reproducible; every
   replacement is generated as ISOLATED untrusted code with its
   granted surface pinned; the differential evaluation runs the
   incumbent and the replacement over the SAME population with both
   sides' outcomes digest-recorded.
3. A REAL end-to-end integration run (env-gated) drives the
   equivalence corpus over the REAL platform path: the REAL replay
   populations serve the differential inputs, the equivalence
   verdicts append to the REAL candidate lifecycle, and the runs are
   measured by the REAL accounting rails.
4. Mechanical verification per row: provenance completeness (the
   verdict cites its proposal and the full differential population),
   criterion explicitness (the acceptance criterion is stated AND
   checked), divergence honesty (a failing case is recorded as a
   divergence, never smoothed), stage discipline (equivalence-verified
   only, never shadow/canary/promoted) — probed adversarially (a
   broken provenance chain, a partial population, an unchecked
   criterion, a skipped stage and a containment escape each FAIL
   mechanically).
5. Honest economics and latency (measured on the live rows,
   none-reported honestly offline); payload digests (never payloads)
   in evidence.
6. Discrimination tests prove the equivalence testing against
   controlled fakes (broken provenance, partial population, unchecked
   criterion, divergence-smoothing, skipped stage, containment escape).
7. Evidence document records REAL outcomes, timings, costs, NOT RUN
   boundaries and the executed battery honestly.
