## Objective

Deliver the production-style pilot with sustained observation: operate the
validated platform the way a production customer would operate it — a declared
pilot window over the recorded application portfolio and the end-to-end
customer journey (VAL-050), driven through the REAL platform path under
production-style operating discipline: a declared schedule of shifts, budgets
and policies enforced for the whole window, incidents in the window handled
through the recorded failure/continuation machinery (a resumed shift
continues exactly once — never dropped, never double-driven), and the
customer boundary respected across the entire window. Sustained observation:
the pilot's per-segment economics, latency and quality are observed against
the audited recorded basis (VAL-049) with drift honestly classified
(within declared bounds / drifting with the mechanism named / regressing
reported as regressing — never silently normalized away).

The pilot composes the RECORDED corpora (digest-referenced, never
re-measured) where its shifts replay recorded workloads — the VAL-050
journey stages with their audited economics carried as the pilot's cost
basis; pure derivation over RECORDED results, a re-measurement masquerading
as pilot observation FAILs. The window's end-of-window accounting reconciles
stage-for-stage, shift-for-shift, against the recorded basis; an unexplained
pilot-level residual FAILs.

The verification core: a pilot that cherry-picks its observation window,
silently normalizes drift, hides an incident or a regressing segment, drops
a scheduled shift, double-drives a resumed shift, leaks the customer
boundary across the window, or reconciles its end-of-window accounting with
a hidden residual FAILs mechanically (each probed adversarially in the
discrimination battery).

## Acceptance criteria

1. The application rides the public SDK boundary with a
   repository-reproducible, secret-free configuration and executable
   tests; the corpus declares, per row, the pilot shape (its observation
   window, its schedule of shifts and their recorded input digests), the
   observation family under test and the expected verdict.
2. Deterministic fixtures make every offline row reproducible; recorded
   results are referenced by content digest (never copied); the window's
   declared recorded basis reconciles exactly where declared.
3. A REAL end-to-end integration run (env-gated) drives one REAL live
   pilot slice — live dispatches sustained across the declared live window
   through the REAL platform path, recorded through the REAL recorder with
   honest economics.
4. Mechanical verification per row: window honesty (the declared
   observation window matches the recorded data — no cherry-picked
   sub-window, omitted segments named), schedule completeness (every
   scheduled shift lands exactly once — missed and duplicated shifts
   named), continuation exactly-once across the window, drift-classification
   honesty (per-segment observation vs the recorded basis — a silently
   normalized drift or a hidden regression FAILs with the segment and
   mechanism named), incident honesty (every incident recorded and
   attributed — a hidden or unattributed incident FAILs named),
   budget/policy envelope integrity (reservations settle, no unauthorized
   spend, the append-only ledger holds), end-of-window accounting
   reconciliation (no hidden residual) and customer-boundary integrity
   across the window — probed adversarially (a window-cherry-picking row,
   a dropped-shift row, a double-driven-resume row, a drift-normalizing
   row, an incident-hiding row, a residual-hiding row and a boundary-leak
   row each FAIL).
5. Honest economics and latency (measured on live rows, none-reported
   honestly offline); payload digests (never payloads) in evidence.
6. Discrimination tests prove the pilot machinery against controlled fakes
   (a cherry-picked window, a dropped shift, a duplicated resume, a
   normalized drift, a hidden incident, a hidden residual, a boundary
   leak).
7. Evidence document records REAL outcomes, timings, costs, NOT RUN
   boundaries (each with its gating env var named) and the executed
   battery honestly.
