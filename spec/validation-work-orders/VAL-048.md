## Objective

Deliver the cross-workload competitive benchmark: the synthesis comparing
Zeck's recorded economics against the alternatives across the complete
workload portfolio. Compose the recorded competitive evidence — the
direct-provider control runs (VAL-041), the strong manually optimized
non-Zeck baseline (VAL-042), the competing gateway/router/agent-stack
benchmark (VAL-043), the adjusted-cost models (VAL-044), the savings
attribution (VAL-045), the substrate economics (VAL-046) and the
longitudinal cost curves (VAL-047) — into the per-workload-class
competitive comparison: the ranking of Zeck against each alternative on
quality-adjusted, latency-adjusted and failure-adjusted cost, with
statistical confidence carried honestly (paired comparisons over the
recorded distributions where the evidence is paired, Wilson 95% intervals,
enforced minimum samples per comparison, and multiple-comparison honesty —
the declared workload portfolio is the recorded portfolio, no cherry-picked
subset, no silent pooling of incomparable units).

Every comparison cites its full recorded evidence population by content
digest (never copied, never re-priced); the cost basis of every leg is
pinned and never silently switched mid-comparison; the paired structure of
the recorded evidence (same workload, same acceptance gate, same recorded
window) is preserved — an unpaired statistic over paired evidence FAILs.

The verification core: a comparison that cherry-picks its workload subset,
pools incomparable units, silently switches its cost basis, discards the
paired structure, inflates confidence beyond its evidence, or claims a
verdict below the enforced minimum samples FAILS mechanically (each probed
adversarially in the discrimination battery).

## Acceptance criteria

1. The application rides the public SDK boundary with a
   repository-reproducible, secret-free configuration and executable
   tests; the corpus declares, per row, the recorded input digests (the
   VAL-041 controls, the VAL-042 baseline runs, the VAL-043 competing
   runs, the VAL-044 adjusted-cost records, the VAL-047 curves), the
   workload class, the comparison family under test and the expected
   verdict.
2. Deterministic fixtures make every offline row reproducible; recorded
   results are referenced by content digest (never copied); every
   comparison's cost basis is pinned and verified; the paired structure of
   the recorded evidence is preserved (never silently pooled or split).
3. A REAL end-to-end integration run (env-gated) drives one REAL
   competitive slice with live dispatches: the comparison computed over
   measured facts, recorded through the REAL recorder with honest
   economics.
4. Mechanical verification per row: portfolio honesty (the declared
   workload portfolio matches the recorded portfolio — no cherry-picked
   subset), unit comparability (no pooling of incomparable units),
   cost-basis integrity (never switched mid-comparison), paired-statistics
   correctness, minimum-sample enforcement, multiple-comparison honesty —
   probed adversarially (a subset-cherry-picking, unit-pooling,
   basis-switching, unpaired-statistics or below-minimum row each FAILS).
5. Honest economics and latency (measured on live rows, none-reported
   honestly offline); payload digests (never payloads) in evidence.
6. Discrimination tests prove the comparison families against controlled
   fakes (subset cherry-picking, unit pooling, cost-basis switching,
   unpaired statistics over paired evidence, confidence inflation,
   sample starvation).
7. Evidence document records REAL outcomes, timings, costs, NOT RUN
   boundaries and the executed battery honestly.
