## Objective

Deliver the shadow slice: the mechanical engine that takes the
equivalence-verified replacements (VAL-033's differentially-evaluated
candidates, read-only input) and runs them IN THE SHADOW — every
shadow execution drives the incumbent path AND the deterministic
replacement path over the SAME live workload traffic through the REAL
platform path, the replacement's outcomes are recorded but NEVER
served to the customer (the incumbent's outcome is always the served
one), and the run produces the REGRESSION COMPARISON: the per-case
shadow-vs-incumbent outcome agreement, the measured latency and cost
deltas, and the divergence ledger — every shadow divergence is
recorded case-by-case with its digests, never smoothed, and never
allowed to affect the served outcome.

The verification core is the shadow oracle: a shadow execution whose
served outcome came from the replacement (the shadow leaking into the
serving path) FAILS mechanically — the shadow is observation-only; a
shadow comparison drawn from a PARTIAL traffic population (dropped
cases, or a population that does not match the recorded workload mix)
FAILS; a regression claim without the full per-case evidence (an
asserted aggregate divergence or an asserted agreement from a subset)
FAILS; and the lifecycle discipline holds — a shadowed candidate
advances to the `shadow-executed` stage ONLY, with every transition
evidenced (canary and promotion are VAL-035's scope — a skipped-stage
advance FAILS).

The isolation discipline carries into the shadow: the replacement
still runs inside its granted isolation surface (an escape during
shadow execution is a containment violation that FAILS and is
recorded); the shadow's telemetry is digest-only (payload bytes never
journaled); and the incumbent's accounting stays honest — the shadow
execution's cost is measured SEPARATELY (the customer is never
billed for the shadow), and a run that books shadow cost onto the
served accounting FAILS.

## Forbidden

- `src/**` and `spec/**` changes;
- applications importing `src/**`;
- mutating any frozen baseline artifact, recorded trajectory, the
  candidate registry's EXISTING entries or the lifecycle's EXISTING
  transitions (VAL-030..033's registries, ledgers and lifecycle
  records are read-only inputs; the shadow run APPENDS its stage
  transition, it never rewrites history);
- SERVING any replacement outcome to the customer path (the shadow
  is observation-only — a leaked shadow outcome FAILS);
- advancing any candidate past the `shadow-executed` stage (canary/
  promotion are VAL-035's scope — a skipped-stage advance FAILS);
- booking shadow cost onto the served accounting rails;
- credentials in the repository, logs or reports;
- fabricated model results or fabricated shadow comparisons;
- recording payload bytes in evidence (digest references only).

## Acceptance criteria

1. The application rides the public SDK boundary with a
   repository-reproducible, secret-free configuration and executable
   tests; the corpus declares, per row, the shadowed candidate (the
   VAL-033 lifecycle identity), the traffic population (the workload
   mix the shadow replays), the expected shadow comparison (agreement
   / honest divergence mix) and the expected stage transition.
2. Deterministic fixtures make every offline row reproducible; every
   shadow execution drives BOTH paths (incumbent + replacement) over
   the same traffic with the served outcome pinned to the incumbent;
   the divergence ledger records every case-by-case divergence with
   digests.
3. A REAL end-to-end integration run (env-gated) drives the shadow
   corpus over the REAL platform path: the REAL recorded traffic
   serves the shadow population, the shadow stage transition appends
   to the REAL candidate lifecycle, the shadow cost is measured
   separately from the served accounting, and the regression
   comparison is recorded over REAL SQL.
4. Mechanical verification per row: serving isolation (the served
   outcome is ALWAYS the incumbent's — a leaked shadow outcome
   FAILS), population completeness (the shadow comparison covers the
   full traffic population), regression honesty (per-case evidence,
   never smoothed aggregates), cost separation (shadow cost measured
   apart, never billed), stage discipline (shadow-executed ONLY,
   evidenced transitions) — probed adversarially (a leaked outcome, a
   dropped case, a smoothed aggregate, a billed shadow and a
   skipped-stage advance each FAIL mechanically).
5. Honest economics and latency (measured on the live rows,
   none-reported honestly offline); payload digests (never payloads)
   in evidence.
6. Discrimination tests prove the shadow execution against controlled
   fakes (leaked shadow outcome, dropped case, smoothed aggregate,
   billed shadow cost, skipped stage, containment escape mid-shadow).
7. Evidence document records REAL outcomes, timings, costs, NOT RUN
   boundaries and the executed battery honestly.
