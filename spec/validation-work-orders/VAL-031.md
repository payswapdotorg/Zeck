# VAL-031 — Repeated Workload Replay and Trajectory Analysis

**Status:** AUTHORIZED / PENDING (eligible now: VAL-030 complete — the frozen baselines this slice replays exist; longitudinal-chain owner: Lead session A per the d63a77e coordination note; surfaces `benchmarks/validation/platform/**` + `apps/workload-replay/**`)
**Program:** Zeck Validation Roadmap
**Assurance:** HIGH_ASSURANCE
**Dependencies:** VAL-003, VAL-004, VAL-005, VAL-007, VAL-030

## Objective

Deliver the repeated-replay slice: the mechanical engine that replays
the frozen baseline corpus (VAL-030's pinned manifests) N times over
the REAL platform path with learning still INERT, captures every
replay's full trajectory through the REAL recorder, and analyzes the
trajectory population for the pre-learning facts every later learning
claim must account for — per-workload step-count stability, the
trajectory-digest distribution across identical replays (which rows
are deterministic across runs and which legitimately vary), the
latency distribution (measured, never estimated), and the accounting
totals per replay (the VAL-006 discipline over the REAL accounting
rails).

The verification core is the replay-population oracle: a replay that
shoulders INTO an existing trajectory identity (a duplicate ledger
identity for one workload's Nth replay) FAILS; a captured trajectory
that is NOT a member of its row's pinned equivalence class (VAL-030's
freeze) FAILS; a stability claim without the full population's
evidence (an asserted distribution from a partial replay set) FAILS;
and the analysis is honest about variance — a row that varies across
identical replays is REPORTED as varying, never smoothed into a fake
determinism claim.

## Acceptance criteria

1. The application rides the public SDK boundary with a
   repository-reproducible, secret-free configuration and executable
   tests; the corpus declares, per row, the pinned baseline manifest
   entry (from VAL-030's registry), the replay count N, and the
   expected trajectory-class membership + population statistics the
   analysis derivation must reproduce.
2. Deterministic fixtures make every offline row reproducible; the
   replay population is recorded through the REAL recorder with one
   immutable trajectory identity per replay (the VAL-007/030 ledger
   discipline).
3. A REAL end-to-end integration run (env-gated) drives the pinned
   replay corpus over the REAL platform path: every replay completes,
   its trajectory is captured, the per-workload populations land in
   the longitudinal ledger, and the accounting totals are measured by
   the REAL accounting rails.
4. Mechanical verification per row: population completeness (exactly N
   replays, no duplicates, no gaps), trajectory-class membership (each
   replay's digest in the row's pinned class), stability honesty
   (deterministic rows show identical digests; varying rows are
   reported varying with their observed distribution), accounting
   honesty — probed adversarially (a duplicated replay identity, a
   missing replay, an out-of-class trajectory, a partial-population
   stability claim and a smoothed variance each FAIL mechanically).
5. Honest economics and latency (measured on the live rows,
   none-reported honestly offline); payload digests (never payloads)
   in evidence.
6. Discrimination tests prove the population analysis against
   controlled fakes (duplicate replay, dropped replay, drifted
   trajectory, partial population, fabricated determinism).
7. Evidence document records REAL outcomes, timings, costs, NOT RUN
   boundaries and the executed battery honestly.
