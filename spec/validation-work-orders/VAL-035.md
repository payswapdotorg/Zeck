## Objective

Deliver the canary slice: the mechanical engine that takes the
shadow-executed candidates (VAL-034's lifecycle records, read-only
input) and drives the governed PROMOTION path — a canary execution
where the replacement serves a pinned, gradually-ramped TRAFFIC
SLICE under explicit admission policy (every canary decision cites
its policy: the ramp schedule, the failure-budget, the divergence
tolerance), the served outcome switches to the replacement ONLY for
the canary slice (the incumbent still serves the remainder), and
every canary step is compared against the recorded shadow evidence
(the shadow's per-case agreement becomes the canary's prior).

The verification core is the promotion oracle: a canary that promotes
WITHOUT the full evidenced lifecycle (a candidate that skipped
shadow-executed, or whose shadow walk is broken) FAILS; a canary step
whose observed divergence exceeds its pinned failure-budget FAILS and
the run ROLLS BACK (the served traffic reverts to the incumbent
 — rollback is mechanical and complete, never partial); a promotion
decision that cites no policy (an unstated ramp/budget/tolerance) or
a policy that was never checked FAILS; the reversibility contract
holds end-to-end (per the deterministicization contract: production
promotion must be reversible to the previous execution plan — the
run records the rollback plan and PROVES it by exercising it); and
the lifecycle discipline holds — the canary advances `shadow-executed
→ canaried → promoted` one evidenced rung at a time (a jump FAILs; an
unpromoted candidate never serves beyond its canary slice).

The safety discipline carries through: the canary slice's outcomes
are accounted SEPARATELY (the canary's cost is measured apart — a
canary cost billed as ordinary served traffic without its marker
FAILs); the replacement still runs inside its granted isolation
surface (an escape during canary execution FAILs); tenant boundaries,
policy admission and budgets are never bypassed by the promotion (a
canary that serves a tenant outside its granted slice FAILs); and the
evidence remains digest-only.

## Forbidden

- `src/**` and `spec/**` changes;
- applications importing `src/**`;
- mutating any frozen baseline artifact, recorded trajectory, the
  candidate registry's EXISTING entries or the lifecycle's EXISTING
  transitions (VAL-030..034's records are read-only inputs; the
  canary run APPENDS its stage transitions, it never rewrites
  history);
- serving the replacement beyond the canary slice before promotion
  (an unpromoted candidate serving full traffic FAILS);
- promoting without the full evidenced lifecycle (a skipped stage
  FAILS);
- an unevidenced rollback (a rollback plan that was never exercised
  FAILS);
- bypassing policy admission, tenant boundaries or budgets;
- credentials in the repository, logs or reports;
- fabricated model results or fabricated canary comparisons;
- recording payload bytes in evidence (digest references only).

## Acceptance criteria

1. The application rides the public SDK boundary with a
   repository-reproducible, secret-free configuration and executable
   tests; the corpus declares, per row, the canaried candidate (the
   VAL-034 lifecycle identity with its shadow evidence), the pinned
   ramp schedule + failure-budget + divergence tolerance, the traffic
   population and the expected outcome (clean promotion / honest
   budget-breach rollback / honest refusal).
2. Deterministic fixtures make every offline row reproducible; every
   canary step serves its slice through the REAL serving path with
   the incumbent serving the remainder; the rollback plan is recorded
   and EXERCISED on every breach (and on demand for the reversal
   probe).
3. A REAL end-to-end integration run (env-gated) drives the canary
   corpus over the REAL platform path: the REAL recorded traffic
   serves the ramp, the canary/promoted transitions append to the
   REAL candidate lifecycle, the canary cost is measured separately,
   and the promotion/rollback decisions are recorded over REAL SQL.
4. Mechanical verification per row: lifecycle completeness (the full
   evidenced walk up to shadow-executed), policy explicitness (the
   ramp/budget/tolerance stated AND checked per step), breach honesty
   (an observed divergence beyond budget FAILs the step and triggers
   the complete mechanical rollback), slice isolation (an unpromoted
   candidate never serves beyond its slice; a tenant outside the
   granted slice FAILs), reversibility (the rollback is exercised and
   complete — served traffic reverts, never partial), stage discipline
   (canaried → promoted one evidenced rung; jumps FAIL) — probed
   adversarially (a skipped lifecycle, an unchecked policy, a
   smoothed breach, an over-slice serve, a partial rollback and a
   mid-canary escape each FAIL mechanically).
5. Honest economics and latency (measured on the live rows,
   none-reported honestly offline); payload digests (never payloads)
   in evidence.
6. Discrimination tests prove the canary promotion against controlled
   fakes (skipped lifecycle, unchecked policy, smoothed breach,
   over-slice serve, partial rollback, mid-canary containment escape).
7. Evidence document records REAL outcomes, timings, costs, NOT RUN
   boundaries and the executed battery honestly.
