# VAL-050 — End-to-End Customer Journey

**Status:** AUTHORIZED / PENDING (eligible now: VAL-049 complete — the audited economics exist; alternation: session B lane per the alternation rule — Lead session A delivered 049)
**Program:** Zeck Validation Roadmap
**Assurance:** HIGH_ASSURANCE
**Dependencies:** VAL-002, VAL-010, VAL-019, VAL-049

## Objective

Deliver the end-to-end customer journey: the complete customer lifecycle
driven through the REAL platform path — onboarding, intent submission,
plan construction, daily usage across the recorded application portfolio
(the VAL-010 text apps and the VAL-019 broad suite), and the
outcome/outtake — every stage's evidence recorded honestly through the
REAL recorder with the audited economics (VAL-049) carried as the
journey's cost basis. The journey composes the RECORDED corpora
(digest-referenced, never re-measured) where its stages replay recorded
workloads; journey-level integrity is mechanical: no dropped stage (a
journey missing a stage FAILs with the stage named), no orphaned state
(every submitted intent lands exactly one durable execution — cross-stage
idempotency verified), no cross-tenant/customer-boundary leak (the
customer boundary respected at every stage), continuation across failure
(a mid-journey failure resumes exactly once, never duplicates), and the
end-to-end latency/cost accounting reconciles stage-for-stage against the
recorded stage economics (an unexplained journey-level residual FAILs).

The verification core: a journey that silently drops a stage, leaves
orphaned state, double-drives a resumed stage, leaks the customer
boundary, or reconciles its end-to-end accounting with a hidden residual
FAILs mechanically (each probed adversarially in the discrimination
battery).

## Forbidden

- `src/**`, `spec/**`, `benchmarks/validation/platform/**`,
  `benchmarks/validation/apps/shared/**` and any other application's
  directory changes (additive and disjoint only; importing existing modules
  is fine);
- applications importing `src/**`;
- re-running or re-pricing recorded inputs (pure derivation over RECORDED
  results where stages replay recorded workloads; a re-measurement
  masquerading as journey derivation FAILs);
- credentials in the repository, logs or reports;
- fabricated model results;
- recording payload bytes in evidence (digest references only).

## Acceptance criteria

1. The application rides the public SDK boundary with a
   repository-reproducible, secret-free configuration and executable
   tests; the corpus declares, per row, the journey shape (its stages and
   their recorded input digests), the integrity family under test and the
   expected verdict.
2. Deterministic fixtures make every offline row reproducible; recorded
   results are referenced by content digest (never copied); the stage
   economics reconcile exactly where declared.
3. A REAL end-to-end integration run (env-gated) drives one REAL live
   journey slice with live dispatches through the REAL platform path,
   recorded through the REAL recorder with honest economics.
4. Mechanical verification per row: stage completeness, cross-stage
   idempotency (no orphaned or double-driven state), continuation
   exactly-once, customer-boundary integrity at every stage, end-to-end
   accounting reconciliation (no hidden residual) — probed adversarially
   (a dropped-stage row, an orphaned-state row, a double-driven-resume
   row, a boundary-leak row and a residual-hiding row each FAIL).
5. Honest economics and latency (measured on live rows, none-reported
   honestly offline); payload digests (never payloads) in evidence.
6. Discrimination tests prove the journey machinery against controlled
   fakes (a dropped stage, an orphaned state, a duplicated resume, a
   boundary leak, a hidden residual).
7. Evidence document records REAL outcomes, timings, costs, NOT RUN
   boundaries and the executed battery honestly.
