# VAL-044 — Quality-Adjusted, Latency-Adjusted and Failure-Adjusted Cost

**Status:** AUTHORIZED / PENDING (dispatch blocked on VAL-041 + VAL-042 + VAL-043 — all three control arms must be complete so the adjusted-cost synthesis compares recorded real results)
**Program:** Zeck Validation Roadmap
**Assurance:** HIGH_ASSURANCE
**Dependencies:** VAL-041, VAL-042, VAL-043

## Objective

Deliver the adjusted-cost synthesis: the comparability verdicts the economic
validation exists for. Compose the three control arms' recorded results
(VAL-041 direct-provider, VAL-042 optimized baseline, VAL-043 competing
stack) with the platform's own recorded runs into the three adjusted-cost
families — QUALITY-ADJUSTED cost (measured cost per unit of verified
quality attainment — never a quality-inflated denominator), LATENCY-ADJUSTED
cost (cost weighted by latency-budget compliance — a latency-omitting
comparison FAILS), and FAILURE-ADJUSTED cost (cost per successfully resolved
outcome with retries and failed attempts fully amortized — the VAL-006
measured-facts-only discipline, NULL when nothing resolved). Every
adjusted comparison carries Wilson confidence, minimum sample sizes, the
pinned price revisions of its inputs, and pre-registered arm sets — no
post-hoc arm or run exclusion.

The verification core: an adjusted comparison that inflates quality in its
denominator, omits latency, hides failures or conflates estimates with
measurements FAILS mechanically (each probed adversarially in the
discrimination battery). The synthesis is a PURE derivation over the
recorded arm corpora (digest-referenced inputs — never copied); live rows
(env-gated, BYOK, measured) drive one REAL adjusted comparison over a REAL
representative slice with every arm priced at its pinned manifest revision.

- credentials in the repository, logs or reports;
- fabricated model results;
- recording payload bytes in evidence (digest references only).

## Acceptance criteria

1. The application rides the public SDK boundary with a
   repository-reproducible, secret-free configuration and executable
   tests; the corpus declares, per row, the recorded arm inputs (digest
   references to the VAL-041/042/43 corpora + the platform runs), the
   adjusted-cost family under test and the expected comparability verdict.
2. Deterministic fixtures make every offline row reproducible; recorded
   arm results are referenced by content digest (never copied); every
   input's pinned price revision is verified against the VAL-040 manifests.
3. A REAL end-to-end integration run (env-gated) drives one REAL adjusted
   comparison over a REAL representative slice with live dispatches: the
   adjusted families computed over measured facts, the verdict recorded
   through the REAL recorder with honest economics.
4. Mechanical verification per row: quality-attainment honesty (the
   denominator is the verified attainment — never inflated), latency
   compliance inclusion, failure amortization completeness (retries and
   failed attempts counted), estimate/measure separation, confidence and
   minimum-sample enforcement — probed adversarially (a quality-inflated,
   latency-omitting, failure-hiding, estimate-conflating or
   below-minimum comparison each FAIL).
5. Honest economics and latency (measured on live rows, none-reported
   honestly offline); payload digests (never payloads) in evidence.
6. Discrimination tests prove the adjusted families against controlled
   fakes (quality inflation, latency omission, failure hiding, estimate
   conflation, post-hoc exclusion, sample-size violation, re-measurement
   masquerade).
7. Evidence document records REAL outcomes, timings, costs, NOT RUN
   boundaries and the executed battery honestly.
