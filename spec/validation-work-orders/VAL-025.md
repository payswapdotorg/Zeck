# VAL-025 — Concurrency, Load, Endurance and Soak Validation

**Status:** AUTHORIZED / PENDING
**Program:** Zeck Validation Roadmap
**Assurance:** HIGH_ASSURANCE
**Dependencies:** VAL-020, VAL-021, VAL-022, VAL-023, VAL-024

## Objective

Deliver the concurrency/load/endurance/soak slice: an application
exercising the platform's concurrency semantics end to end —
same-key concurrent submissions arbitrated to EXACTLY ONE durable
execution per idempotency key (the ledger-key discipline proven in
VAL-018/021 extended to the RACING case: two submissions in flight
simultaneously, the ledger admits one, the duplicate observes the
winner's outcome or a typed already-in-flight rejection — never two
executions); distinct-key parallelism (independent submissions proceed
concurrently without head-of-line blocking, verified by observed
overlap or interleaved journal ordering); bounded-concurrency load
shaping (submissions beyond the declared ceiling receive the typed
throttled/queue-full rejection at admission — the REAL platform
vocabulary — with zero effects); and endurance/soak (repeated
submission rounds over a sustained window with the durable invariants
re-verified after every round: journal exactly-once-per-attempt, no
ledger drift, no row-count leak, idempotency keys never arbitrating a
second transition) — over the REAL platform path with the REAL
ledger/journal as the arbitration authority.

## Acceptance criteria

1. The application rides the public SDK boundary with a
   repository-reproducible, secret-free configuration and executable
   tests; the corpus declares, per row, the submission pattern
   (same-key race, distinct-key fan-out, over-ceiling burst, soak
   rounds), the expected admission outcomes and the expected durable
   state (executions, journal rows, ledger transitions) after the
   pattern settles.
2. Deterministic fixtures make every offline row reproducible
   (scripted arbitration outcomes where the platform's own scheduler
   decisions are the variable under observation are still verified
   against the platform's REAL invariants — never scripted around
   them).
3. A REAL end-to-end integration run (env-gated) drives the pinned
   corpus over the REAL platform path: same-key races resolve to
   exactly one durable execution (ledger-verified); distinct-key
   fan-outs complete independently; over-ceiling bursts observe the
   typed admission rejection with zero effects; the soak window
   re-verifies the durable invariants after every round.
4. The concurrency invariants are verified mechanically per row:
   exactly-once-per-key arbitration, journal exactly-once-per-attempt,
   no phantom executions, no orphan ledger transitions, and the
   terminal ↔ criteria ↔ journal reconciliation invariant
   (anyFail→FAILED) probed adversarially.
5. Honest economics and latency (per-submission and per-round);
   payload digests (never payloads) in evidence.
6. Discrimination tests prove the invariants against controlled fakes
   (double-arbitration, journal double-write, phantom execution,
   orphan transition, ceiling bypass).
7. Evidence document records REAL outcomes, timings, the declared
   soak window, NOT RUN boundaries and the executed battery honestly.
