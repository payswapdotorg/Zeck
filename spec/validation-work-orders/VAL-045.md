## Objective

Deliver the attribution slice: the mechanical engine that takes the
recorded economics (VAL-044's quality/latency/failure-adjusted cost
model, read-only input) and the determinization lifecycle's recorded
history (VAL-030..036's registries, ledgers, lifecycle records and
maturity reports, read-only input) and attributes the measured savings
to their mechanisms — per workload family, per promotion generation:
which savings came from REUSE (the cross-workload shape candidates),
which from CACHE (the stable input→output transforms), which from
COMPETENCE (the recurring step-pattern tools), which from
DETERMINISTICIZATION (the displaced model rounds), and which residual
savings remain UNATTRIBUTED (honestly reported, never forced into a
mechanism) — every attribution claim citing its evidence (the exact
ledger entries, lifecycle records and cost measurements it attributes
from), and the attribution reconciling exactly: the sum of attributed
savings + the honestly-unattributed residual EQUALS the recorded total
savings (a non-reconciling attribution FAILs; a double-counted
mechanism FAILs; a savings attributed to two mechanisms
simultaneously FAILs).

The verification core is the attribution oracle: an attribution claim
with an uncited or phantom evidence population FAILs; an attribution
that double-counts (the same ledger entry attributed to two
mechanisms) FAILs with both claims named; an attribution that forces
the residual into a mechanism (an unattributed savings claimed as
reuse/cache/competence/deterministicization without its mechanism's
recorded evidence) FAILs; the counterfactual discipline holds — the
attributed savings per mechanism must be measured against the
recorded incumbent baseline (the cost the mechanism actually
displaced, per VAL-044's adjusted model), never against a
hypothetical or favorable baseline (an attribution against an
unstated or swapped baseline FAILs); and the maturity linkage is
honest — the attribution's generation series must match the maturity
benchmark's recorded generations (VAL-036's curves; a gapped or
mismatched generation series FAILs).

## Forbidden

- `src/**` and `spec/**` changes;
- applications importing `src/**`;
- mutating any frozen baseline artifact, recorded trajectory, the
  candidate registry's EXISTING entries, the lifecycle's EXISTING
  transitions or the maturity reports (VAL-030..036's records are
  read-only inputs; the attribution run APPENDS its report, it never
  rewrites history);
- fabricating or extrapolating measurements;
- forcing the unattributed residual into a mechanism;
- credentials in the repository, logs or reports;
- recording payload bytes in evidence (digest references only).

## Acceptance criteria

1. The application rides the public SDK boundary with a
   repository-reproducible, secret-free configuration and executable
   tests; the corpus declares, per row, the workload family, the
   generation series (the recorded evidence it attributes over), the
   pinned mechanism evidence (which ledger entries/lifecycle records
   belong to which mechanism) and the expected attribution (the
   per-mechanism split + the honest residual).
2. Deterministic fixtures make every offline row reproducible; every
   attribution cites its full evidence population with digests; the
   reconciliation identity (attributed + residual = recorded total)
   holds per family and per generation.
3. A REAL end-to-end integration run (env-gated) drives the
   attribution corpus over the REAL platform path: the REAL recorded
   economics + lifecycle history serve the attribution, the
   attribution reports append over REAL SQL, and the runs are
   measured by the REAL accounting rails.
4. Mechanical verification per row: evidence-citation completeness,
   no-double-count (each ledger entry attributed to at most one
   mechanism), residual honesty (the unattributed residual reported,
   never forced), counterfactual fidelity (the recorded baseline,
   per VAL-044's adjusted model), generation-series integrity
   (matching VAL-036's curves) — probed adversarially (an uncited
   claim, a double-count, a forced residual, a swapped baseline and a
   gapped series each FAIL mechanically).
5. Honest economics and latency (measured on the live rows,
   none-reported honestly offline); payload digests (never payloads)
   in evidence.
6. Discrimination tests prove the attribution against controlled
   fakes (uncited claim, double-count, forced residual, swapped
   baseline, gapped series).
7. Evidence document records REAL outcomes, timings, costs, NOT RUN
   boundaries and the executed battery honestly.
