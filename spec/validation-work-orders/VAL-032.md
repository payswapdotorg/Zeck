# VAL-032 — Learned Reuse/Cache/Competence/Deterministicization Discovery

**Status:** AUTHORIZED / PENDING (eligible now: VAL-031 complete — the replay-population facts exist; longitudinal-chain owner: Lead session A per the d63a77e coordination note; surfaces `benchmarks/validation/platform/**` + `apps/learning-discovery/**`)

## Objective

Deliver the discovery slice: the mechanical engine that mines the
recorded trajectory populations (VAL-031's replay ledger) for
LEARNABLE structure — repeated sub-trajectory shapes across workloads
(reuse candidates), recurring input→output transformations with
stable digests (cache candidates), workload families whose competence
step-patterns recur (competence candidates) and workload segments
whose outcomes are stable across identical replays and therefore
determinizable (deterministicization candidates) — and records every
discovery as a PROPOSAL in the deterministicization lifecycle's
candidate registry (the VAL-004/005 comparator + candidate grammar),
never as an applied change.

The verification core is the discovery oracle: a proposal must cite
its evidence population (the exact trajectory digests + replay
identities it generalizes from — an uncited proposal FAILS); a
candidate derived from a PARTIAL population (a stability claim from
an incomplete replay set) FAILS; a proposal whose cited digests are
not members of the recorded population (a fabricated evidence
citation) FAILS; and the discovery is conservative — a varying
segment (VAL-031's honestly-reported variance) is NEVER proposed as a
deterministicization candidate (a variance-smoothing proposal FAILS).

## Acceptance criteria

1. The application rides the public SDK boundary with a
   repository-reproducible, secret-free configuration and executable
   tests; the corpus declares, per row, the mined evidence population
   (trajectory digests + replay identities from VAL-031's ledger),
   the candidate kind (reuse/cache/competence/deterministicization)
   and the expected proposal the discovery derivation must emit (or
   honestly refuse to emit).
2. Deterministic fixtures make every offline row reproducible; every
   proposal is recorded as a lifecycle candidate (the VAL-004/005
   grammar) with its evidence citation, never as an applied change.
3. A REAL end-to-end integration run (env-gated) drives the discovery
   corpus over the REAL platform path: the replay ledger's REAL
   populations are mined, proposals land in the REAL candidate
   registry, and the discovery run's accounting is measured by the
   REAL accounting rails.
4. Mechanical verification per row: evidence citation completeness
   (every cited digest is a member of the recorded population; every
   generalization cites its full population), candidate-kind fidelity
   (the proposal's kind matches the mined structure), conservatism
   (varying segments are never proposed as deterministicization
   candidates) — probed adversarially (an uncited proposal, a partial
   population, a fabricated citation and a variance-smoothing
   proposal each FAIL mechanically).
5. Honest economics and latency (measured on the live rows,
   none-reported honestly offline); payload digests (never payloads)
   in evidence.
6. Discrimination tests prove the discovery against controlled fakes
   (uncited proposal, partial population, fabricated citation,
   variance-smoothing proposal, state-mutating proposal).
7. Evidence document records REAL outcomes, timings, costs, NOT RUN
   boundaries and the executed battery honestly.
