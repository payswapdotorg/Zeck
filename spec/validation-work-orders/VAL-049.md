## Objective

Deliver the anti-gaming audit: prove the economic results are
REPRODUCIBLE and UNGAMED. Two arms. (1) REPRODUCIBILITY: replay the
recorded economic evidence end-to-end — re-derive every adjusted-cost,
attribution, substrate and cross-workload verdict from the recorded
inputs (digest-verified) and prove bit-stable recomputation (a verdict
that flips on replay FAILS); re-run a sampled live subset on the REAL rail
and prove the recorded economics fall within their declared confidence
bounds (a live re-run landing outside its own bounds FAILS — the
measurement was noise, not signal). (2) ANTI-GAMING: probe the economic
machinery adversarially — every gaming vector the discrimination
batteries warned about (quality inflation, latency omission, failure
hiding, estimate conflation, post-hoc exclusion, window cherry-picking,
regime normalization, hidden weights, silent drops, re-measurement
masquerade) is re-probed against the FINAL integrated machinery at once,
plus cross-vector combinations (a row that passes each probe in isolation
but games the combination FAILS). The audit verdict per finding:
REPRODUCIBLE-VERIFIED / BOUNDS-HELD / GAMING-DETECTED (with the exact
mechanism) / NOT-AUDITABLE (honest boundary, with reason).

The verification core: an audit that rubber-stamps without re-derivation,
samples only favorable subsets, or reports gaming-detected findings as
resolved without a fix or an accepted-risk record FAILS mechanically.

- fabricated model results;
- recording payload bytes in evidence (digest references only).

## Acceptance criteria

1. The application rides the public SDK boundary with a
   repository-reproducible, secret-free configuration and executable
   tests; the corpus declares, per row, the audited input digests, the
   audit arm under test and the expected audit verdict.
2. Deterministic fixtures make every offline row reproducible; audited
   inputs are referenced by content digest (never copied); re-derivation
   is bit-stable where declared.
3. A REAL end-to-end integration run (env-gated) drives one REAL live
   re-run audit slice with live dispatches: the sampled re-runs' measured
   economics checked against the recorded bounds, recorded through the
   REAL recorder with honest economics.
4. Mechanical verification per row: replay bit-stability (or the honest
   divergence record with cause), bounds-held checking (live re-run vs
   declared bounds), gaming-probe completeness (every vector + cross-vector
   combinations), verdict-grounding (each verdict cites its evidence) —
   probed adversarially (a rubber-stamp row, an off-bounds row reported
   as held, a combination-gaming row each FAIL).
5. Honest economics and latency (measured on live rows, none-reported
   honestly offline); payload digests (never payloads) in evidence.
6. Discrimination tests prove the audit machinery itself against
   controlled fakes (a flipped verdict on replay, an out-of-bounds
   re-run, an undetected gaming vector, a combination-gaming case).
7. Evidence document records REAL outcomes, timings, costs, NOT RUN
   boundaries and the executed battery honestly.
